## 4. H1 recursive pass

### H1-CANON-01 — evaluator regime is materially under-typed

The paper asks:

"When canonical Jewish interpreters make incompatible factual claims, what gives a
third-party evaluator grounds to favor one as true?"

The route formalization is explicitly evaluator-indexed:

RouteGround_e(H_i,tau).

RECOGNIZE then requires evaluator e to have warranted grounds for identifying,
authenticating, and relying on the discriminator.

But the frozen architecture does not declare the relevant evaluator regime.

The same project also states that the scholar can analyze emic truth-directed
structures second-order without personally adjudicating the theological truth of the
rival claims.

These roles can diverge.

Examples of materially different evaluator roles include:

- a religious insider who accepts the authority system's background commitments;
- a later traditional evaluator who accepts some but not all authority premises;
- a scholar performing etic second-order analysis;
- a generic epistemic agent asked what evidence warrants belief.

A route can be recognizable and usable for one evaluator while failing for another.

Therefore the free variable e is not merely cosmetic. It can change route success.

Effect on current fixed case:

LOW for the current bounded negative result because every admitted Jewish route stops
at TRACE before RECOGNIZE becomes case-dispositive.

Effect on positive control and general method:

HIGH.

The prophecy positive control and any general claim about evaluator-usable grounds
depend on what e is licensed to recognize as warrant.

Spine localization:

Q -> B, especially RECOGNIZE, with consequences for C_method and manuscript framing.

Suggested paper repair:

Make evaluator-indexing explicit.

The manuscript can distinguish:

- the emic evaluator relative to whom a religious route could supply grounds; and
- the etic scholar who analyzes whether the route has that structure without thereby
  endorsing the religious premise.

Do not let "third-party evaluator" silently switch between these roles.

### H1-CANON-02 — the Jewish case does not itself exercise the downstream gates

Every currently admitted Jewish module stops at TRACE:

- H_P: TRACE
- H_M: TRACE
- H_A: TRACE
- H_X: TRACE
- H_R: TRACE
- H_L: TRACE
- H_G: TRACE

Therefore the fixed Jewish case strongly tests source-to-target traceability and
target typing.

It does not contain an actually instantiated Jewish route that passes TRACE and then
fails or passes DISCRIMINATE or RECOGNIZE.

This matters because the architecture also advances the three-gate structure as a
general route-level characterization.

The later gates are currently supported by:

- hostile deletion/collapse tests;
- conceptual analysis;
- external epistemic-authority literature;
- exploratory cross-tradition structural tests;
- an in-principle prophecy control.

Those are legitimate support types, but they are not the same evidence as the fixed
Jewish case.

Effect on current bounded case result:

NONE.

Effect on C_method and publication presentation:

HIGH.

Suggested paper repair:

Explicitly separate two claims:

1. CASE RESULT:
   the selected Jewish routes fail before a usable target trace is established;

2. GENERAL METHOD:
   once target traceability is available, discriminatory force and evaluator warrant
   remain distinct additional jobs.

Do not imply that the Rashi-Ramban case itself empirically validates all three gates.

### H1-CANON-03 — disciplinary generalization needs type control

The architecture's broad derived language includes:

"Religious authority is functionally differentiated and target-relative rather than
epistemically portable by status alone."

The research object itself is careful elsewhere:

- the Jewish source inventory is purposive and non-exhaustive;
- cross-tradition tests are exploratory structural holdouts, not independent
  validation;
- universal-negative claims are prohibited.

The strongest evidence directly establishes that the audited authority/knowledge
structures perform different native jobs and that status alone does not license
transfer to this fixed truth target.

That supports a strong methodological principle.

It does not, by itself, establish an unrestricted empirical universal about all
religious authority.

Effect on current case and method:

NONE.

Effect on C_disciplinary and journal-facing framing:

MEDIUM TO HIGH.

Suggested repair options:

- bound the empirical wording: "Across the audited structures...";
- or state the broader result as a methodological requirement:
  religious-authority claims must be typed by function and target before epistemic
  transfer is inferred.

The latter preserves the stronger contribution with less overgeneralization risk.

### H1-CANON-04 — the prophecy control is in-principle, not realized

H_X is correctly recorded as:

"positive_control_successful_in_principle_but_target_coverage_not_instantiated."

The manuscript contract asks for a positive-control mechanism showing that
traditional adjudication is not ruled out by definition.

This works as a logical or architectural positive control.

It does not supply an actually successful route on I_S.

Suggested guard:

Use "in-principle positive control" or equivalent language.
Do not present H_X as a realized Jewish adjudication of the fixed case.

This is a wording/presentation control rather than a defect in the current
architecture.

## 5. H4 representation-aware localization

The H1 findings were tested across four representations:

1. stable argument spine:
   A -> Q -> H[F] -> R -> B -> C;

2. reader-facing method:
   PROFILE -> TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE, with COMPOSE when needed;

3. source-module result table;

4. contribution layers:
   C_case, C_method, C_disciplinary, C_publication.

### H4-CANON-01 — evaluator under-typing is robust

The evaluator issue persists across:

- Q, where the "third-party evaluator" is introduced;
- B, where RECOGNIZE is evaluator-relative;
- C_method, where evaluator-usable grounds become part of the general method.

It does not require changes to:

- the Rashi-Ramban collision;
- the admitted source modules;
- their current TRACE failures;
- the bounded no-completed-route result.

Therefore the smallest robust repair frontier is:

Q + RECOGNIZE/B + C_method/manuscript explanation.

A global source or case rebuild is not licensed.

### H4-CANON-02 — case/method evidence asymmetry is robust

The module-result representation shows all seven Jewish routes stop at TRACE.

The reader-facing method representation contains three route gates.

The contribution-layer representation advances both a bounded case result and a
general method result.

Therefore the distinction between case evidence and method evidence is not an artifact
of one representation.

The robust repair is an evidence-status distinction, not deletion of DISCRIMINATE or
RECOGNIZE.

### H4-CANON-03 — disciplinary overbreadth is representation-localized

The source and case representations are already bounded.

The overbreadth risk appears primarily in the C_disciplinary / derived-result wording.

Therefore the correct repair is local claim calibration.

The core architecture does not need to be weakened.

## 6. H2 non-routed comparator

H2 was run independently as a representation/counterfactual comparator.

### H2-CANON-01 — RECOGNIZE compresses two potentially non-equivalent jobs

The current research controls explicitly combine:

- evaluator warrant; and
- social recognizability

inside a reader-facing RECOGNIZE/use stage.

But these can vary independently.

A socially recognized authority can be epistemically unwarranted for the target.
An evaluator can have strong epistemic grounds for an evidential relation that is not
socially recognized as authoritative.

Therefore social recognizability cannot silently substitute for warrant.

The route-level gate is safe only if RECOGNIZE means warranted epistemic
identifiability/usability, with social recognizability treated as one possible
diagnostic or mechanism rather than a universal sufficient condition.

This was the strongest unique comparator yield.

Spine localization:

B / RECOGNIZE and manuscript method explanation.

### H2-CANON-02 — selection-effect warning independently confirms H1-CANON-02

Because every admitted Jewish route stops at TRACE, the Jewish case disproportionately
tests the first gate.

The later gates are supported by other reasoning and stress tests.

This independently confirms the need to distinguish:

- case demonstration;
- conceptual method decomposition;
- cross-tradition structural support.

### H2-CANON-03 — evaluator counterfactual confirms H1-CANON-01

Substituting different evaluator types changes what can count as warranted recognition
without altering the source structure itself.

That is direct result-sensitivity evidence that evaluator type is a live coordinate.

## 7. H5 advisory repair audit

No live Canonical file was edited.

The minimum non-regressive repair package is:

### REPAIR-1 — type the evaluator

Before manuscript drafting hardens the terminology, declare who evaluator e is or
state that the method is evaluator-relative.

Make the etic scholar / emic evaluator distinction explicit.

### REPAIR-2 — separate case evidence from method evidence

State that the fixed Jewish case strongly establishes repeated TRACE failure across
the admitted structures.

Present DISCRIMINATE and RECOGNIZE as additional necessary jobs in the general method,
supported by conceptual analysis, hostile tests, relevant literature, and exploratory
structural tests rather than by successful downstream passage in the fixed case.

### REPAIR-3 — calibrate the disciplinary claim

Prefer:

"the audited authority structures perform different functions, so epistemic transfer
must be target-relative and explicitly licensed"

over an unrestricted empirical claim that all religious authority is intrinsically
target-relative in the same way.

A broader claim can remain a hypothesis or methodological thesis if clearly typed.

### REPAIR-4 — protect warrant from social-recognition substitution

Define RECOGNIZE in epistemic terms.

Social recognizability can support or mediate the gate, but social status alone does
not supply warrant.

This is especially important because the paper's broader thesis already argues that
status categories do not become truth evidence merely by being authoritative.

### REPAIR-5 — preserve the positive-control label

H_X is an in-principle positive control.

Do not upgrade it into an actually instantiated case selector.

## 9. Paper-usefulness verdict

The experiment produced four manuscript-relevant controls:

1. evaluator typing;
2. case-evidence versus method-evidence separation;
3. disciplinary-scope calibration;
4. clarification of RECOGNIZE so social recognition cannot replace epistemic warrant.

These are useful before drafting because all four can be repaired at the architecture
or framing level without rewriting a finished manuscript.

## 10. G2U router verdict

### Did the routed sequence produce useful yield?

YES.

H1 found:

- evaluator under-typing;
- case/method evidence asymmetry;
- disciplinary claim overbreadth risk;
- positive-control wording control.

H4 showed that the first three are local repairs and do not require rebuilding the
case or source modules.

### Did the non-routed comparator add unique yield?

YES.

H2 added the strongest unique representation result:

RECOGNIZE can lose information if evaluator warrant and social recognizability are
treated as equivalent.

H2 also independently confirmed evaluator result-sensitivity and the case/method
evidence asymmetry.

### Router implication

The router prediction was useful but incomplete for the broader publication-risk
objective.

For a narrowly specified hidden-bridge objective:

H1 -> H4 -> H5

remains supported.

For a hidden-bridge plus manuscript-representation-risk objective, this experiment
supports the candidate composition:

(H1 || H2) -> H4 -> H5

where H1 and H2 are run independently before synthesis.

This is a G2U research finding, not yet a canonical router update.
