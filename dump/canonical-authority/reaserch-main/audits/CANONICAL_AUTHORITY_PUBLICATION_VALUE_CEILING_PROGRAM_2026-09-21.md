## Publication-value control

Publication value is treated as a qualitative constrained optimization problem.

Raise one or more of:

- novelty;
- importance;
- rigor;
- generality;
- evidential support;
- clarity;
- reproducibility;
- reader recoverability.

Control the costs of:

- scope drift;
- conceptual complexity;
- research burden;
- reviewer attack surface;
- word-count burden;
- unsupported generalization.

A ceiling move is valuable only when its strict gain survives those costs.

Result:
the first five-condition COMPOSE candidate compressed.

Surviving candidate:

1. inputs are successful target-indexed route grounds;
2. the composition specification is independently licensed;
3. unqualified selection is invariant across the materially admissible specification
   set;
4. non-selection remains a lawful output.

New distinction:

- kappa-relative selection;
- robust all-things-considered selection.

Formal pattern:

`K_e(G,tau) = {kappa | Adm_e(kappa | G,tau)}`.

A rival receives an unqualified strict selector only when every materially admissible

initial five-condition proposal.

## Recursive ceiling result: meta-selector closure

After the eight-part program, a further hostile recursion asked whether selecting
among admissible COMPOSE rules merely generates an infinite hierarchy of meta-rules.

Result:

the regress terminates by attribution and non-forcing rather than by positing a final
neutral selector.

For every outcome-material selector coordinate, the architecture requires one of:

1. source attribution;
2. explicit independently justified evaluator contribution with corresponding claim
   indexing;
3. invariance of the asserted conclusion across materially admissible alternatives.

Otherwise the strongest warranted output remains relative, partial, tied, or
unresolved.

This strengthens PV-2 and PV-1 without adding another reader-facing stage.

## Net result

All eight publication-value moves produced usable gains.

No move required changing:

- the fixed case;
- the canonical goals;
- the A->Q->H[F]->R->B->C spine;
- the current bounded no-selector result.

The largest strict gain is the COMPOSE successor plus selector-attribution result.

The largest rigor gain is the combined hostile-test, prospective-holdout, and
article-facing source-closure package.

The largest clarity gain is reader-level compression.

The largest novelty gain is not a new generic aggregation theorem. It is the
integration of source-grounded authority transfer, route-level discrimination,
evaluator recognizability, composition, and selector attribution around a fixed
religious truth target.

## New credible ceiling

The paper can now plausibly aim to make three nested contributions from one case:

1. Jewish philosophy:

2. Philosophy of religious authority:

3. General methodology:
   an attribution-sensitive distinction between source-route grounds and the

   verdict.

The third contribution is the principal ceiling raise generated by this program.
