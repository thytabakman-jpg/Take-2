# Canonical Authority Reader-Dependency and Publication-Value Compression Audit

## Objective

Raise publication value through clarity, explanatory compression, and reduced reviewer
attack surface without changing the fixed spine or removing any load-bearing result.

Protected research architecture:

`A -> Q -> H[F] -> R -> B -> C`

Protected route-level jobs:

typed source reconstruction, TRACE, DISCRIMINATE, RECOGNIZE.

Protected higher-level result:

route-level success does not automatically yield all-things-considered selection.

## Compression principle

The repository needs explicit machinery for recoverability and regression testing.

The article needs only enough machinery for a reader to reconstruct and test the
argument.

Therefore research-coordinate count and manuscript-concept count are different
optimization targets.

## Reader-facing method

### Do not lead with five capitalized stages

PROFILE is required setup, not an independent success conjunct.

In article prose, PROFILE can normally be expressed as:

"First reconstruct what the source structure actually authorizes, transmits,
settles, authenticates, or explains in its native setting."

Then introduce only the three route-level tests as the reusable method:

1. TRACE: what source-grounded path connects that native output to the exact disputed
   proposition?
2. DISCRIMINATE: does the path support one rival rather than the other concerning
   truth?
3. RECOGNIZE: what grounds does the later evaluator have for identifying and using
   that discriminator?

This preserves all route-level work with lower conceptual load.

### Delay COMPOSE

COMPOSE need not appear before the reader understands the fixed case.

The paper can first establish the route-level result. Only after showing what would
count as a successful route does it expose the second problem:

even several successful routes do not automatically produce one case-level verdict.

That is the natural point to introduce composition.

The reader-visible COMPOSE result can remain compact:

- route grounds are already target-indexed;
- the composition rule itself needs independent grounds;
- an unqualified verdict requires stability across the materially admissible
  composition rules;
- unresolved or tied output remains legitimate.

Weights, dependence structures, thresholds, priorities, defeat relations, and PD
sensitivity machinery remain under the hood unless an example makes one load-bearing.

## Source-module presentation

Seven modules need not become seven method tutorials.

Use one common comparison frame, then group the source material by native job while
preserving individual source conclusions.

### Family A: legitimacy, settlement, and institutional procedure

- H_P pluralism/determination
- H_A authority/error

Reader payoff:
binding or legitimate settlement does not automatically become evidence that one
fixed descriptive rival is true.

### Family B: provenance, transmission, loss, and hierarchy

- H_M protected reception
- H_L loss/reconstruction
- H_G generational hierarchy

Reader payoff:
provenance, memory, reconstruction, or comparative stature can alter what an evaluator
knows about a tradition without themselves supplying the missing proposition-specific
selector.

### Family C: privileged or case-proximate truth access

- H_X authenticated prophecy
- H_R Ramban creation tradition

Reader payoff:
these are the strongest positive-control/case-proximate possibilities because their
native functions can be truth-directed, but they still require an instantiated TRACE
to the exact target.

This grouping compresses exposition without collapsing module identities.

## Positive-control placement

Place H_X near the point at which the method might otherwise look definitionally
hostile to religious adjudication.

Its job is not to add another failed route.

Its job is to establish that the method allows a traditional authority mechanism to
succeed when:

1. the privileged source is authenticated;
2. its content covers the fixed target;
3. the content discriminates the rivals;
4. the evaluator has grounds to recognize that relation.

That protects the method from a major reviewer objection at low word-count cost.

## Broader payoff derived from the Jewish case

The broader contribution must follow by dependency rather than being appended.

### Step 1

The Jewish modules demonstrate that things ordinarily grouped under "authority" have
different native outputs:

- legitimacy;
- legal settlement;
- provenance;
- transmission;
- authentication;
- hierarchy;
- explanation;
- privileged access.

### Step 2

Because those outputs differ, "authority" cannot be treated as one portable
epistemic quantity.

### Step 3

A truth-directed third-party use therefore requires an explicit source-to-target
relation, discriminatory effect, and evaluator warrant.

### Step 4

Even successful route-level grounds do not settle the case when several routes
conflict.

### Step 5

The composition rule can itself contribute selecting information.

Therefore a final verdict can contain two analytically distinct sources of selection:

- what the religious sources contribute;
- what the evaluator's composition rule contributes.

### General implication

Religious authority is target-relative and attribution-sensitive.

The methodological contribution is not merely that different authority types exist.
It is that a scholar can compare heterogeneous authority structures at one fixed
truth target while preserving native function and tracking exactly where
truth-discriminating information enters the inference.

## Candidate one-sentence contribution

A religious authority structure gives a later evaluator usable grounds in a fixed
truth dispute only through a source-attributable path that discriminates the rivals
and is warrantedly recognizable; where several such grounds conflict, an
all-things-considered verdict additionally depends on a separately licensed
composition rule whose own selector contribution must not be attributed back to the
sources.

## Candidate article dependency path

1. Fixed Rashi-Ramban collision.
2. Third-party question.
3. Why familiar Jewish authority mechanisms are tempting answers.
4. Minimal route test: TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE after source reconstruction.
5. Source-module comparison organized by native job.
6. Positive control.
7. Bounded current result.
8. General authority-transfer result.
9. Multi-route COMPOSE and hidden-selector attribution result.
10. Bounded novelty and scope.

## Material to keep out by default

- PD framework name and equations;
- twenty-field and thirty-field scan history;
- full candidate registers;
- every diagnostic coordinate;
- every hostile counterexample;
- all composition mathematics unless required for the venue;
- GitHub governance;
- extensive cross-tradition material;
- journal-optimization machinery.

These remain available for footnotes, reviewer response, or revision.

## Publication-value effect

### Novelty

The composition-attribution result becomes easier to see because it is not buried
under the discovery machinery.

### Importance

The broader implication follows directly from a real Jewish case rather than being
asserted as an abstract theory of authority.

### Rigor

Every reader-visible inference remains backed by the richer repository controls.

### Generality

The fixed-target method extends structurally beyond the Jewish case without requiring
the article to become a comparative survey.

### Evidential support

The primary-source and prospective-holdout controls remain available beneath the
compressed presentation.

### Clarity

The reader learns three route-level questions before encountering the separate
case-level composition problem.

### Scope/complexity cost

Reduced.

## Compression verdict

PASS.

No protected result is lost.

The strongest reader-facing architecture is not five coequal stages. It is:

`source reconstruction -> TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE`

for route-level grounds, followed only when necessary by:

`COMPOSE`

for case-level selection.

The composition theory itself can remain mostly implicit unless the final venue and
novelty claim warrant formal presentation.
