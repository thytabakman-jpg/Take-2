## Status

Workstream: `WS:2026-09-21:CANON-COMPOSE-20-FIELD-CEILING`

Baseline architecture being tested: `CANON:ARCH:001 v1.1.0`

`PROFILE -> TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE`

with separate case-level `COMPOSE` when multiple successful routes materially conflict.

## Holdout

Sikh authority centered on the Guru Granth Sahib and the relation between scriptural
Guru authority, interpretive application, and communal/institutional authority.

This tradition was not one of the four prior exploratory cross-tradition stress tests.

## Prediction frozen before literature inspection

1. No new route-level primitive will be required beyond typed setup, TRACE,
   DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE.
2. Merely establishing canonical or Guru status will not by itself establish a
   proposition-specific truth discriminator for an arbitrary contested target.
3. Any move from scriptural authority to a later proposition will require an
   inspectable source-to-target bridge rather than status portability.
4. Where scriptural and communal/institutional authority both bear on the same target,
   route-level success and case-level composition will remain distinct.
5. A case in which the relevant Sikh authority structure yields practice, identity,
   legitimacy, or settlement rather than proposition-specific truth support will be
   typed as such rather than forced into an adjudicative success category.
6. The holdout will either preserve the architecture or expose a genuinely omitted
   semantic job. No post hoc primitive may be added merely to rescue the framework.

## Failure conditions

The architecture fails this holdout if the literature reveals a material authority
operation required for third-party truth-directed assessment that:

1. cannot be represented as typed source setup;
2. cannot be represented as a licensed source-to-target TRACE;
3. supplies adjudicative force without DISCRIMINATE;
4. is usable by the evaluator without any RECOGNIZE analogue; or
5. requires case-level selection among conflicting successful routes but cannot be
   represented by a separately licensed COMPOSE/defeat relation.

Any failure will be preserved rather than repaired before classification.
