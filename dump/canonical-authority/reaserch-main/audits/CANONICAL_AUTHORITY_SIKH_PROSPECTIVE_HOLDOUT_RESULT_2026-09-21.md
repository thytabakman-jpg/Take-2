## Observed authority structure

### Scriptural Guru authority

The Guru Granth Sahib is treated not merely as a respected text but as Guru and a
normative/supratextual source of authority. Its functions described in the literature
include personal piety, liturgy, ceremonies, communal solidarity, identity, and
normative authority.

### Interpretation

The authority of the text does not collapse interpretation into one mechanically
available output. The literature describes multiple Sikh hermeneutic schools and
historically situated interpretive practices.

### Institutional authority

The Akal Takht functions as a central Sikh institution of authority. The reviewed
literature explicitly distinguishes legitimacy of the institution from authority of
particular officeholders and treats the jathedar as fallible rather than as a
revelatory oracle. Authority is historically and socially mediated.

## Prediction test

### Prediction 1: no new route-level primitive

PASS.

The observed material can be represented using typed source setup, TRACE,
DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE. No additional semantic job is required.

### Prediction 2: canonical/Guru status is not itself proposition-specific discrimination

PASS.

The literature attributes several native functions to Guru Granth Sahib authority.
Those functions do not by themselves yield a discriminator for every possible
third-party factual target.

### Prediction 3: source-to-target bridge remains necessary

PASS.

Multiple hermeneutic traditions show that authoritative textual status does not make
later interpretive application transparent. A target-specific relation still has to
be established.

### Prediction 4: route success and composition remain distinct

PASS at structural level.

Sikh scriptural, interpretive, communal, and institutional authority are distinguishable
authority loci. Where more than one supplies target-relevant grounds, combining them
would require an additional relation rather than following automatically from their
status.

### Prediction 5: native outputs remain typed

PASS.

The literature explicitly exhibits outputs involving identity, communal solidarity,
normative practice, legitimacy, and institutional authority. The framework does not
need to redescribe each as proposition-specific truth evidence.

### Prediction 6: preserve failures without rescue

PASS.

No post-inspection primitive was added to fit the holdout.

## Holdout verdict

The preregistered holdout preserves the current route-level architecture and
independently strengthens four claims:

1. religious authority is functionally differentiated;
2. authority status is not epistemically portable to an arbitrary fixed target;
3. authoritative source status and interpretive application are distinct;
4. multiple authority loci make the separate COMPOSE problem structurally realistic.

## Limitation

This is a prospective structural holdout, not a second full fixed-proposition case.
It provides stronger anti-overfitting evidence than the earlier exploratory holdouts
because the architecture and failure conditions were frozen before inspection, but it
does not establish universal external validity.
