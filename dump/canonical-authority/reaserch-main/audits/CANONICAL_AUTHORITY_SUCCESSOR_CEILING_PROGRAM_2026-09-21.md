## I. Deletion tests

### PROFILE deletion

Question:
Can PROFILE be removed as an independent adjudicative success condition?

Result:
Yes, as a success conjunct; no, as a semantic job.

Reason:
A valid TRACE already requires a typed source node and a native output. Without
identifying what H is and what it natively supplies, TRACE is not well-formed.
Therefore PROFILE is best treated as required setup for TRACE rather than as a fourth
independent condition of adjudicative success.

Counterexample to eliminating the job entirely:
An analyst sees "canonical authority" and directly maps the status to descriptive
truth without establishing whether the source natively outputs legitimacy, legal
bindingness, provenance, authentication, memory, or truth-directed evidence.
The error is blocked either by an explicit PROFILE operation or by building the same
typing requirement into TRACE.

Disposition:
Compress PROFILE into mandatory typed setup while retaining it procedurally for
reader clarity.

### TRACE deletion

Question:
Can a route adjudicate without a licensed source-attributable path from native output
to the fixed target?

Result:
No.

Hostile countermodel:
H supplies only institutional legitimacy. Independent modern philological evidence E
supports P_N. If TRACE is deleted, the augmented system H + E can discriminate P_N
and the evaluator can recognize E, but the selector would be falsely attributed to H.

Disposition:
TRACE is independently necessary for source-attributable authority-based
adjudicative contribution.

### DISCRIMINATE deletion

Question:
Can a traced and recognized authority relation adjudicate when it does not
asymmetrically bear on the rivals?

Result:
No.

Hostile countermodel:
H validly establishes that both P_R and P_N are legitimate canonical interpretations.
The path is source-grounded and recognizable, but it provides no truth-directed
difference between the rivals.

Disposition:
DISCRIMINATE is independently necessary.

### RECOGNIZE deletion

Question:
Can a genuine objective discriminator give the third-party evaluator usable grounds
when the evaluator lacks warranted access to or authentication of it?

Result:
No for the current Q.

Hostile countermodel:
An inaccessible authenticated oracle possesses a reliable discriminator favoring P_N,
but the later evaluator has no grounds for knowing that the oracle existed, that its
message covered I_S, or that the recovered report is authentic.

Disposition:
RECOGNIZE is independently necessary for evaluator-usable adjudication. It is not
necessary to the bare existence of an objective discriminatory relation.

## II. Collapse tests

### PROFILE versus TRACE

PROFILE does not survive as an independent success conjunct.
It is a prerequisite for a well-typed TRACE and can be represented as setup.

### TRACE versus DISCRIMINATE

They do not collapse.

A source-to-target relation can be licensed yet symmetric, non-truth-directed, or
insufficiently specific to distinguish P_R from P_N.

### DISCRIMINATE versus RECOGNIZE

They do not collapse.

A discriminator can exist without being warrantedly identifiable by evaluator e.

### TRACE versus RECOGNIZE

They do not collapse.

A later evaluator can recognize that an authority structure exists and is socially
legitimate while lacking a licensed proposition-specific path from its native output
to the fixed target.

## III. Route-level sufficiency test

Let tau = {P_R, P_N}.

After typed source setup, define a route-level usable ground:

RouteGround_e(H_i,tau)
iff
there exists Delta_i^tau such that

1. TRACEABLE_Hi,tau(Delta_i^tau);
2. DISCRIMINATING_tau(Delta_i^tau);
3. RECOGNIZABLE_e(Delta_i^tau).

Within the declared meanings of traceability, discriminatory truth-directed force,
and warranted recognizability, these three jobs are jointly sufficient for saying
that H_i supplies evaluator-usable pro tanto grounds favoring one rival.

This is a conditional analytic characterization, not yet a theorem establishing one
universal substantive theory of evidence, authority, or warrant.

## IV. Case-level sufficiency failure and composition requirement

The route-level characterization is not sufficient for an all-things-considered case
selection when several successful routes conflict.

Hostile countermodel:

- H_1 supplies a traceable, discriminating, recognizable Delta_1 favoring P_N.
- H_2 supplies a traceable, discriminating, recognizable Delta_2 favoring P_R.
- Both pass the route-level test.
- No rule has been supplied for weighting, defeating, ranking, or otherwise composing
  the two route contributions.

Therefore:

RouteGround_e(H_i,tau)
does not imply
CaseSelect_e({H_i},tau)
for arbitrary multi-route sets.

A separate case-level operation is required:

COMPOSE_A({Delta_i})

where A is a declared, independently licensed, non-outcome-driven aggregation or
defeat relation.

Candidate case-level characterization:

CaseSelect_e(Hset,tau)
iff
COMPOSE_A({Delta_i | RouteGround_e(H_i,tau)})
produces a strict warranted ordering of the rivals.

The current Rashi-Ramban corpus does not activate this problem because no admitted
route yet reaches a complete usable discriminator. COMPOSE is therefore a general
architecture requirement but not currently load-bearing to the bounded negative case
result.

## V. Refined architecture

The strongest surviving representation is now two-level.

### Route setup and route characterization

SETUP / PROFILE:
reconstruct H_i and its typed native output.

Then:

TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE.

These are the three independent route-level success jobs.

### Case-level selection

Successful route contributions are inputs to a separate:

COMPOSE / DEFEAT

operation when more than one route contributes materially.

This yields a cleaner distinction:

objective/source-attributable discriminatory relation
!=
evaluator-usable route ground
!=
all-things-considered case selection.

## VIII. External cross-tradition holdout stress tests

These are exploratory structural holdouts, not claims of independent universal
validation.

### Holdout 1: Roman Catholic papal/magisterial infallibility

Structure:
Centralized institutional teaching authority with expressly delimited domains and
conditions.

Observed stress:
Official Catholic materials distinguish classes of teaching and restrict infallible
teaching to defined conditions and subject domains such as faith and morals.

Architecture result:
- SETUP is indispensable because "the Pope says X" is too coarse.
- TRACE asks whether the particular act is of the authority type claimed and whether
  its protected domain reaches the target proposition.
- DISCRIMINATE asks whether the teaching actually selects one rival.
- RECOGNIZE asks whether the evaluator has grounds for classifying the act correctly.

Result:
Strong support for target-relative and scope-sensitive TRACE.

Primary anchor:
Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, Doctrinal Commentary on the Concluding
Formula of the Professio fidei (1998).

### Holdout 2: Sunni hadith criticism

Structure:
Distributed transmission/authentication practices involving isnad, matn, expert
criticism, and differing juristic/traditionist methods.

Observed stress:
Modern scholarship documents meaningful differences between traditionist and jurist
methods and between isnad-centered and matn/external-criterion assessment.

Architecture result:
- SETUP must identify what "authentication" method and output are at issue.
- TRACE from authenticated transmission to "the Prophet said X" differs from TRACE
  to "the factual content X is true" or "X determines law."
- DISCRIMINATE remains target-relative.
- RECOGNIZE includes expert access to transmitter and textual criticism.

Result:
Strong cross-tradition support for separating authentication/provenance from content
truth and downstream normative use.

Anchors:
Mutaz al-Khatib, "Hadith Criticism between Traditionists and Jurisprudents" (2020).
Jonathan Brown, "How We Know Early Hadith Critics Did Matn Criticism..." (2008).

### Holdout 3: Pentecostal/charismatic prophecy

Structure:
Charismatic claimed revelation with communal, pastoral, individual, and leadership
discernment.

Observed stress:
Recent Pentecostal scholarship emphasizes that prophecy can perform teaching,
pastoral, ethical, and public functions rather than only prediction, while
discernment can involve individual, communal, and hierarchical accountability.

Architecture result:
- SETUP prevents the label "prophecy" from predetermining a predictive truth function.
- TRACE must connect the particular prophetic function to the target claim.
- DISCRIMINATE is necessary for true/false or rival-claim selection.
- RECOGNIZE is visibly social because communities develop practices for discerning
  source and authority.

Result:
Strong support for native-function typing and evaluator/social recognition.

Anchors:
Wolfgang Vondey, "Synodality and Charisms..." Theological Studies 85 (2024).
Ndidzulafhi Mudau and Rendani Tshifhumulo, review of contemporary Pentecostal
prophecy (2026), used only as a recent illustration of function plurality.

### Holdout 4: Latter-day Saint revelation

Structure:
Central prophetic authority coexisting with personal revelation.

Observed stress:
A 2026 Harvard Center for the Study of World Religions account describes an early
Latter-day Saint distinction between personal revelation and revelation authorized
for the church as a whole.

Architecture result:
- SETUP identifies bearer and scope.
- TRACE blocks transport of a personal revelatory claim into church-wide authority
  without the required authorization relation.
- DISCRIMINATE asks whether the authorized revelation actually decides the target.
- RECOGNIZE asks how the evaluator identifies the authorized church-wide bearer.

Result:
Strong support for scope-sensitive TRACE and bearer recognition.

Anchor:
Center for the Study of World Religions, Harvard Divinity School, "Revelation"
(2026).

### Holdout verdict

The same route jobs remain useful across:
- centralized institutional authority;
- distributed transmission criticism;
- charismatic revelation/discernment;
- centralized prophetic authority plus personal revelation.

No holdout required a new route-level primitive.

The cross-tradition tests did expose the separate COMPOSE problem whenever multiple
successful authorities can conflict.

## X. Ramban-specific source investigation

Primary-text findings from Ramban on Genesis 1:1:

1. Ramban states that the process of creation is a deep mystery not fully knowable
   from the verses and links complete knowledge to a Mosaic tradition.
2. In his criticism of Rashi and subsequent account he separately gives what he calls
   the "simple correct explanation" of Genesis 1:1.
3. Later in the same comment he distinguishes an esoteric "way of truth" reading
   involving higher realities.

A recent peer-reviewed article by Haim Perlmutter describes the majority of Ramban's
Genesis 1:1 comment as a critical discourse with Rashi followed by Ramban's
comprehensive approach to the verse and creation sequence.

Current inference:
The source strongly supports coexistence of received/esoteric creation knowledge and
a peshat-level argument against Rashi. It does not establish that the peshat syntactic
claim I_S is itself received esoteric knowledge.

Therefore the H_R TRACE failure is strengthened, not repaired.
