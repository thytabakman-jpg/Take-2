1. changes the fixed case result;
2. changes the central method characterization;
3. removes a material reviewer vulnerability;
4. produces strict explanatory compression; or
5. raises the credible publication ceiling enough to exceed its added scope,
   complexity, word-count, and attack-surface cost.
