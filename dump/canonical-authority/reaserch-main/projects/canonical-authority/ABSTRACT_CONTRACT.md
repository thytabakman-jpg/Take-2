# Canonical Authority Abstract Contract

Status: working manuscript control
Date: 2026-09-24

## Job

A future submission abstract must compress the paper without importing project knowledge that
is absent from the abstract itself.

The frozen experiment abstract remains unchanged for reproducibility. This contract governs any
later real abstract.

## Required content

A submission abstract must make recoverable, in compact form:

1. the exact fixed disagreement, including enough content to identify the rival proposition rather
   than merely saying "the Rashi-Ramban disagreement over Genesis 1:1";

2. the second-order scope boundary, including that the paper does not itself decide the ultimate
   first-order grammatical verdict;

3. the purposive and non-exhaustive corpus status, with enough indication of the admission basis
   that the seven-module set does not look arbitrary;

4. the distinction between typed source reconstruction and the adjudicative gates TRACE,
   DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE;

5. the fact that package-level admissibility is a condition on joint source use and is not the
   same operation as corpus admission;

6. the positive-control conditions: truth-directed success is possible in principle only where
   target coverage, rival discrimination, and warranted evaluator uptake are present;

7. the actual bounded result: the current case stops at package TRACE because the proposition-
   specific source-to-target edge is not established;

8. the derived target-relative/attribution-sensitive authority implication as a consequence of
   the preceding analysis, not as an independent assertion;

9. COMPOSE only as a conditional downstream requirement when several completed grounds conflict.

## Compression rule

Compression may remove notation, backend governance, source-registry detail, audit history, and
most module-by-module exposition.

Compression may not remove a prerequisite while retaining a conclusion that depends on it.

## Ending rule

Do not compress the bounded case result, broader significance, and conditional composition
boundary into language that makes them appear to be one result of one semantic type.

## Novelty rule

A submission abstract may state only the bounded novelty language supported by the final
nearest-neighbor review. The experiment abstract is not evidence for novelty.

## Verification

Before promotion of any real abstract:

- compare it against RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.yaml for factual/method fidelity;
- compare it against MANUSCRIPT_MOVEMENT_MAP.md for reader dependency;
- run goal-decoupled observation for compression loss;
- run goal-directed regression for claim preservation.
