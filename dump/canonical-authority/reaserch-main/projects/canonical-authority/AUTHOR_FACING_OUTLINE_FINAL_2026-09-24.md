# When Canonical Interpreters Disagree: Source-Grounded Truth Assessment in Rashi and Ramban on Genesis 1:1

Author-facing outline

Status: READY FOR AUTHOR REVIEW  
Date: 2026-09-24

This is the paper plan to approve before we write prose.

It contains the substantive argument and source placement only. Internal controller, audit,
routing, and repository machinery is intentionally omitted.

# Paper goal

Use the fixed Rashi-Ramban disagreement on Genesis 1:1 to ask a narrow second-order question:

When two canonical Jewish interpreters make incompatible factual claims, what source-grounded
basis, if any, can a later evaluator use to favor one claim as true?

The paper does not decide the ultimate Hebrew-grammar question itself.

It asks what selected Jewish structures of interpretation, transmission, authority,
institutional decision, hierarchy, and privileged access can legitimately contribute to that
truth-directed assessment.

# Fixed case

I_S:

Genesis 1:1 has bara Elohim as the main predication rather than as part of a dependent temporal
construction leading to verse 3.

Rashi:

P_R = not I_S.

Ramban:

P_N = I_S.

Current bounded result:

Across the currently admitted source structures, no independently admissible source package
currently establishes the proposition-specific source-to-target link needed to select between
P_R and P_N.

The paper therefore stops at a TRACE obstruction in the worked case.

# Section 4 — From Source to Ground

## Section goal

Present the paper's adjudicative method as simply as possible.

The method has three gates after source reconstruction:

TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE.

## 4.1 TRACE: Does the Source Reach the Target?

Question:

Does an independently admissible source package actually connect its source-grounded content to
the fixed proposition?

A source package can contain one source or several sources.

Point earned:

General relevance is not enough. There must be a licensed source-to-target relation.

## 4.2 DISCRIMINATE: Does the Ground Favor One Rival?

Question:

Does the traced relation favor one rival over the other concerning truth?

Point earned:

A relation can be truth-relevant without being rival-discriminating.

## 4.3 RECOGNIZE: Can the Evaluator Use It?

Question:

Does the later evaluator have warranted grounds to identify, authenticate, and use that
discriminator?

Point earned:

A source-side discriminator and evaluator-usable grounds are distinct.

## 4.4 When Grounds Conflict: The COMPOSE Boundary

Keep this compact.

Several sources may combine inside TRACE to establish one ground.

COMPOSE is a different problem. It arises only when several already-completed grounds exist and
must be combined or defeated.

Point earned:

Package TRACE is not COMPOSE.

Transition:

The method is now clear. The next section first shows that the method can succeed in principle,
then applies it to the actual case.

# Section 6 — Authority Across Targets

## Section goal

Derive the broader philosophical payoff without pretending the case proves a universal theory of
religious authority.

## 6.1 Why Authority Does Not Automatically Transfer

Compare the native functions from Section 3.

A mechanism can be authoritative for:
- legal settlement;
- provenance;
- authentication;
- legitimacy;
- institutional action;

without automatically being authoritative for a different descriptive truth target.

Core claim:

Authority with respect to one target does not automatically transfer to another target.

A licensed target relation is required.

## 6.2 Where Selector Information Comes From

Hold source content fixed.

If a bridge rule or composition rule creates the eventual selection, that information comes from
the added machinery.

Core claim:

A verdict produced by added bridge/composition machinery cannot be attributed to fixed source
content alone.

## 6.3 What the Scholar Can Claim

Explain that the paper can analyze the internal truth-directed structure of Jewish claims without
personally deciding the theological truth of those claims.

Point earned:

The method allows rigorous second-order analysis without collapsing emic and etic levels.

Transition:
