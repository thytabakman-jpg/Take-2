## Tool use between every material step

Not every atomic action reruns the entire stack.

At a material transition:
IC-017 checkpoint -> verify actual delta -> A16 where consequence is material -> IC-011/012 reselection -> Multi-Object relation refresh when another workstream can be affected -> TransferCore when reusable relation appears.

At an atomic operation inside a still-valid selected package:
continue the package without full re-entry.

## Stop rule

Stop research expansion and enter manuscript architecture when:
- W1, W2, W3 are jointly DRAFT_SAFE;
- no demonstrated architecture-changing successor remains under the declared capability/evidence basis;
- remaining OPENs are explicit and non-silent;
- independent challenge fails to expose a blocking dependency;
- A16 passes;
- IC-017 returns a stable local disposition;
- IC-011 selects manuscript architecture rather than another research workstream.
