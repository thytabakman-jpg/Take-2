# 1. Governing objective

Write the paper so that every major claim is earned where its evidence and inferential basis first become sufficient.

Do not treat the backend discovery count as the manuscript contribution count.

The paper has five irreducible contribution bundles:

I1. Fixed-target heterogeneous comparison.

I2. Source-attributable route architecture:
PROFILE -> TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE.

I3. Package TRACE / COMPOSE separation with selector attribution.

I4. Bounded Rashi-Ramban package-TRACE result, protected by a positive control.

I5. Target-relative and attribution-sensitive authority.

The strongest bounded novelty claim is their intersection:

N* = I1 ∩ I2 ∩ I3 ∩ I4 ∩ I5.

This is the paper-level contribution architecture.

# 2. Global writing law

Each contribution must be:

1. introduced only after its prerequisites are visible;
2. stated at the narrowest defensible strength;
3. tied to its source/evidence basis;
4. distinguished from nearby non-equivalent claims;
5. earned before later sections rely on it;
6. carried upward only through a licensed 36-cell transition;
7. restated in Section 7 only as synthesis.

A locally elegant claim cannot become a whole-paper contribution through wording alone.

COMP -> SYS promotion is gated.

A section-level observation becomes a paper-level claim only after:
- source grounding;
- dependency completion;
- hostile distinction testing;
- reader-state verification;
- novelty/boundedness check.

# 4. Discovery disposition rules during drafting

## Promote

A discovery may enter main prose as a contribution claim when:
- it belongs to I1-I5;
- it is needed to earn I1-I5;
- or it is needed to bound/defend the novelty claim.

## Support only

Use supporting discoveries when they:
- exemplify a distinction;
- verify robustness;
- provide a positive control;
- provide source evidence;
- block a predictable objection.

Do not give them independent contribution language.

## Backend/control only

Research-control discoveries remain outside article prose unless manuscript execution reveals them as load-bearing.

Examples:
- tool-use experiments;
- currentness architecture;
- surface synchronization;
- reader-projection control;
- execution routing;
- liveness/termination controls;
- 36-cell writing machinery itself.

These improve the paper without becoming the paper's subject.

## Future/deferred

Keep future-paper and corpus-expansion discoveries outside the article unless independently activated.
