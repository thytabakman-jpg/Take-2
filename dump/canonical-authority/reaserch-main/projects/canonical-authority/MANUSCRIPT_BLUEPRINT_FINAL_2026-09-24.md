# 1. Root goal

## G0

Produce a rigorous, source-grounded peer-reviewed paper using the fixed Rashi-Ramban
disagreement on Genesis 1:1 to determine what selected second-order Jewish epistemic,
interpretive, institutional, transmission, authority, and privileged-access structures can
legitimately contribute to assessing incompatible truth-apt canonical claims.

## Reader-level paper promise

The paper asks a precise third-party question:

When two canonical Jewish interpreters make incompatible factual claims, what source-grounded
basis, if any, can a later evaluator use to favor one claim as true?

The paper does not answer that question by assuming that every religious authority mechanism
does the same job.

Instead it:

1. fixes one exact disputed proposition;
2. reconstructs selected Jewish mechanisms in their native functions;
3. tests whether any licensed source package reaches the disputed proposition;
4. distinguishes source-to-target relevance from actual discrimination and evaluator uptake;
5. applies that method to the Rashi-Ramban case;
6. derives a bounded conclusion and a broader target-relative account of authority transfer.
