# Canonical Authority Manuscript Contract

## Purpose

The research artifact and the reader-facing article perform different jobs.

The repository preserves enough structure, failed paths, provenance, source typing,
open variation, and audit controls to make the research recoverable. The manuscript
contains only what a reader needs to understand, test, and evaluate the argument.

This contract prevents the dossier from becoming the article and prevents elegant
prose from hiding a load-bearing inference.

## Controlling object

The manuscript is derived from RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.yaml.

SPINE.yaml preserves the stable paper identity:

A -> Q -> H[F] -> R -> B -> C.

The compact manuscript notation does not replace the full canonical spine constraint. SPINE.yaml is authoritative for the expanded R pathway, B** = T + L + I + W decomposition, and the commitments attached to the spine nodes.

SPLINE.yaml preserves the reader-facing dependency refinement:

A -> Q -> PROFILE(H[F]) -> TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE
-> [COMPOSE when several completed grounds materially conflict] -> C.

The spline does not replace the spine. It expands reader-visible jobs inside the
stable argument identity. For the current fixed case, the realized evidential path
stops at TRACE and reaches the bounded case result; later gates remain method jobs,
positive-control possibilities, or conditional downstream dependencies rather than
steps the case itself successfully traverses.

RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.yaml contains the current integrated pre-manuscript object:
the fixed case, current method, module outcomes, bounded result, general
contribution, and realized ceiling.

The manuscript may present that structure implicitly or with different prose
headings. It may not silently change the underlying argument or import material
directly from discovery/audit registers without promotion into the integrated
research object.

## Source identity and citation authority

The manuscript uses `research/SOURCE_REGISTRY.yaml` as the stable identity and
verification-status authority for primary and secondary sources.

`research/SOURCE_MODULES.yaml` owns the source's analytic role in the paper.

Historical transfer documents, dossiers, visual maps, and prior audits remain
provenance. A historical file calling itself the "current source of truth" does not
override the current GitHub research object, source registry, or source-module
analysis.

A paper-facing source claim must therefore have both:

1. a resolvable source identity with an appropriate verification status; and
2. an analytic use licensed by the current source-module or architecture layer.

Pending source gates remain visible rather than being silently filled by historical
language.

## Reader-facing burden

The article must make visible:

1. the exact Rashi-Ramban contradiction required by the paper;
2. the third-party adjudication question;
3. why apparently relevant Jewish mechanisms perform different native jobs;
4. the compressed method: PROFILE as typed setup, then TRACE an independently
   licensed source package to the fixed target, DISCRIMINATE whether the traced
   relation asymmetrically supports a rival, and RECOGNIZE whether evaluator e has
   warranted epistemic grounds to use that discriminator;
5. the distinction between package TRACE and all-things-considered case selection:
   several sources may jointly establish one ground, while several completed grounds
   require separately licensed COMPOSE/defeat semantics;
6. at least one positive-control mechanism showing that traditional adjudication is
   not ruled out by definition;
7. the bounded comparative result;
8. the broader implication for religious authority, fixed-target comparison, and
   traceable epistemic transfer;
9. the nearest-neighbor literature required to state novelty without exaggeration.

## Research machinery that normally remains under the hood

The article does not need to display:

- the full historical discovery register;
- every failed branch;
- every PD equation or audit family;
- portfolio governance;
- GitHub control architecture;
- every source considered and rejected;
- every sensitivity or representation test;
- every provenance record;
- every ceiling-raise candidate;
- every reason the inventory remains globally open.

Those controls remain available for source notes, footnotes, reviewer response, or
revision when they become load-bearing.

## PD rule

PD can generate, test, and sharpen distinctions used by the paper.

The article does not present PD as an additional framework unless a later manuscript
decision establishes that doing so passes the ceiling-raise net-gain test.

Current article-visible methodological content uses PROFILE as typed setup and three
route-level gates:

- TRACE;
- DISCRIMINATE;
- RECOGNIZE.

COMPOSE/defeat is visible only to the extent required to distinguish route-level
grounds from all-things-considered case selection; it is not expanded into a second
theory unless the manuscript's general characterization claim requires it.

Finer PD-derived distinctions such as exact target typing, source versus application,
licensed transfer, attribution of added selectors, bearer/representation control,
indexed pluralism, and bounded response-space controls remain available as
diagnostic support rather than separate manuscript-level concepts unless a specific
argument requires them.

## Ceiling-raising rule

A ceiling-raising move enters the article only when:

1. it attaches to an existing spine node;
2. it strengthens the strongest defensible contribution;
3. it does not change the paper's identity;
4. the added scholarly value exceeds its complexity and scope cost.

The candidate and decision register is research/CEILING_RAISE_REGISTER.yaml.
A ceiling raise becomes part of the paper only when it is promoted into
RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.yaml under its realization rule.

## Publication orientation

Current research architecture supports a paper with three nested payoffs.

First, a Jewish-philosophy payoff: a fixed canonical disagreement and a
source-grounded comparison of Jewish epistemic, interpretive, institutional,
transmission, and privileged-access structures.

Second, a philosophy-of-religion payoff: religious authority is target-relative and
requires a licensed transfer from its native achievement to a particular contested
proposition.

Third, a broader religious-studies payoff: heterogeneous religious authority and
knowledge structures can be compared through one fixed truth target by typing their
native outputs, tracing any licensed source-to-target relation, testing discriminatory
effect, and testing evaluator recognizability without flattening the structures into
one natural kind. The architecture also distinguishes a route-level ground from the
additional problem of composing multiple conflicting successful routes.

The broader payoffs must emerge from the worked Jewish case. They cannot be added as
detached claims in the introduction or conclusion.

## Drafting sequence

Draft from the reader spline in SPLINE.yaml, not from research chronology.

Keep the stable argument spine recoverable underneath the presentation and keep the
realized fixed-case path distinct from the general method path.

1. Establish the case and question.
2. Locate the paper against the closest predecessor literature.
3. Introduce only the distinctions required to understand the comparison.
4. Analyze the selected source modules and any independently admissible source
   packages using the same TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE logic after typed setup.
5. Show the positive control.
6. State the bounded case result.
7. Explain briefly why route-level success would still require a separate composition
   rule if successful routes conflicted.
8. Derive the broader authority-transfer and attribution implications.
9. Conclude with scope and open questions.

## Anti-bloat test

For every proposed paragraph ask:

- Which spine node and spline stage does it serve?
- Which inference or reader dependency fails without it?
- Does it raise the ceiling or merely display research?
- Can the reader recover the same argument without it?

Material with no clear answer remains in the research artifact rather than the
manuscript.


## Promoted v1.2.0 translation controls

The following controls were developed in
`research/CEILING_SUCCESSOR_CANDIDATE.yaml` and are now promoted into
CANON:ARCH:001 v1.2.0. The candidate file remains provenance for the promotion.

### Package-aware TRACE

The manuscript must not imply that failure of every singleton authority route proves
failure of every possible source combination.

The successor candidate permits an independently licensed source package to establish
one target-indexed ground.

This remains distinct from COMPOSE:

- package TRACE asks whether several sources jointly establish one ground;
- COMPOSE asks how several already-established grounds are aggregated or defeated.

Relative to the currently reconstructed seven-module corpus, the package audit still
finds no completed TRACE to I_S because no other current module supplies the
proposition-specific edge missing from H_R.

### Evaluator indexing

RECOGNIZE is evaluator-relative and epistemic.

Social recognizability can mediate or support warranted uptake but cannot substitute
for warrant merely because an authority is socially recognized.

The article may distinguish an emic evaluator, relative to whom a religious route can
supply grounds, from the etic scholar who analyzes that structure without thereby
endorsing every religious premise.

### Evidence-layer discipline

The manuscript distinguishes:

1. what the fixed Jewish case directly demonstrates;
2. what the general method requires by conceptual characterization;
3. what hostile tests and holdouts validate;
4. what broader disciplinary implication is derived;
5. what publication positioning is derivative.

Do not present the Rashi-Ramban case as though it independently instantiates successful
passage through every downstream gate.

### Contribution minimality

The default visible article keeps:

- the fixed collision and third-party question;
- native source reconstruction;
- package-aware TRACE;
- DISCRIMINATE;
- evaluator-relative RECOGNIZE;
- the in-principle positive control;
- the bounded result;
- target-relative and attribution-sensitive authority implications;
- a compact COMPOSE boundary.

The default article keeps PD nomenclature, full field-scan history, most hostile
counterexamples, full kappa-space formalism, meta-selector recursion, repository
governance, and publication-optimization machinery in the backend unless a specific
reviewer-facing inference makes one load-bearing.

### Theory-freeze control

After a final post-recovery package-TRACE regression, another theoretical construct is
inserted into the current article only when it changes the case, changes the central
characterization, removes a material reviewer vulnerability, strictly compresses the
visible theory, or raises the credible publication ceiling enough to justify its added
scope and complexity.

Material that fails this test routes to follow-on research rather than into the
current manuscript.

## 2026-09-23 Reader-Epistemic and Section Architecture Addendum

The manuscript is now controlled through four linked objects:

`SPINE -> argument units -> reader transitions -> manuscript sections`

The protected macro spine remains:

`A -> Q -> H[F] -> R -> B -> C`

The reader-facing refinement remains:

`A -> Q -> PROFILE(H[F]) -> TRACE -> DISCRIMINATE -> RECOGNIZE -> [conditional COMPOSE] -> C`

### Reader-transition contract

For every major argument unit record:

starting reader state
-> live problem
-> needed resource
-> first use
-> inference licensed
-> epistemic change
-> search-space change
-> next live problem

This is a manuscript construction control, not a new research theory.

### Current section planning baseline

1. Introduction
2. Fixed Case and Comparison Frame
3. Roads, Native Functions, and Route-Specific Commitments
4. TRACE, DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE
5. Positive Control and Bounded Case Result
6. Conditional COMPOSE / Defeat Boundary
7. Broader Religious-Authority Implication
8. Contribution, Scope, and Conclusion

Each section has one dominant substantive job. Section numbering and headings remain
revisable; the substantive jobs are downstream of the protected spine and reader
transitions.

### Contribution earning

The five major contributions are not treated as five flat claims.

- fixed-target heterogeneous comparison is primarily earned in Section 2;
- TRACE / DISCRIMINATE / RECOGNIZE is earned in Section 4 and demonstrated in Section 5;
- COMPOSE / defeat boundary is earned in Section 6;
- target-relative and attribution-sensitive authority is derived in Section 7;
- the bounded Rashi-Ramban result is earned in Section 5.

Supporting T2 material qualifies or supports these claims. T3 enters only when it changes
or defends a current-paper claim. T4 remains deferred unless it has a demonstrated pathway
back into the current paper.

### Road presentation control

The article need not present every historical response family as a full section. A family
earns visible manuscript space when it supplies a materially different epistemic mechanism,
commitment, or historically important response. Branches earn separate treatment when the
branch distinction changes the commitment, truth-selection relation, or historical
attribution. Bookkeeping variants remain in the research artifact.

### Case/general-method boundary

The current case path stops at TRACE. DISCRIMINATE, RECOGNIZE, and COMPOSE remain general
or conditional method jobs unless the evidence changes the case result. Positive control is
shown so the bounded result is not misread as an impossibility-by-definition claim.
