## Purpose

Translate the seven-section outline into page-sized intellectual movements.

A movement is not a literal fixed page count. It is the smallest reader-visible unit that performs one main argumentative job and produces one material reader-state change.

Unit contract:

U_i =
<Job, ReaderIn, LiveQuestion, Material, Inference, ReaderOut, Pull>

Cut, merge, or move a unit when:
- its job duplicates another unit;
- its material arrives before the reader has a use for it;
- its inference is not earned;
- its ReaderOut does not matter to a later dependency;
- its Pull does not create a real next question.
