## CONTRIBUTION ARCHITECTURE

CA71-01 semantic analysis found that the five retained contribution identifiers are not five same-type or coequal results. They are typed subcomponents of a three-layer paper-facing architecture:

```
CORE METHOD
  C1 fixed-target heterogeneous comparison
    -> C2 TRACE / DISCRIMINATE / RECOGNIZE

RESULTS AND LIMITS
  fixed-case application of C1+C2 -> C5 bounded Rashi-Ramban result
  multiple completed conflicting grounds -> C3 conditional COMPOSE / defeat boundary

DERIVED SIGNIFICANCE
  C1+C2+attribution results -> C4 target-relative and attribution-sensitive authority
```

C1-C5 remain stable traceability labels. This typing prevents tier or contribution language from implying that method, case result, conditional boundary, and disciplinary implication are independent objects of one kind.

## CONTRIBUTION SELECTION RULE

A T3 or T4 item enters the manuscript only when one of these occurs:

- it becomes necessary to establish a T1/T2 claim;
- the literature shows that it materially changes the novelty delta;
- manuscript execution exposes it as load-bearing;
- reviewer-facing defense requires it;
- new evidence changes its result status.

A contribution does not enter merely because it is interesting, well supported, or historically important.
