## Irreducibility and entailment tests

### C1

Not reducible to C2. C2 requires a comparison target and typed source objects; C1 explains why heterogeneous mechanisms can be compared at one target without treating them as one kind of authority.

C1 does not by itself entail C2. Alternative comparison methods remain logically possible.

Disposition: retain as an enabling methodological contribution.

### C2

Survives deletion/collapse testing already recorded in D031-D038 and D054-D057. TRACE, DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE perform distinct jobs. PROFILE remains setup rather than a success conjunct.

Disposition: retain as the central methodological contribution.

### C3

Not part of the realized fixed-case evidential path because the current case stops at package TRACE. It becomes necessary only when several completed grounds materially conflict.

C3 is not entailed by C2 alone. It is triggered by C2-success multiplicity plus conflict.

Disposition: retain as a conditional boundary/extension, but do not present it as a coequal realized result of the fixed case.

### C4

C4 is substantially derivative. Functional differentiation, licensed transfer, selector attribution, and evaluator uptake support the characterization that authority is target-relative and attribution-sensitive.

C4 adds disciplinary compression and generality, so derivation does not make it redundant.

Disposition: retain as derived significance, not as an independent primitive contribution.

### C5

C5 is neither method nor theory. It is the bounded application result: current admitted packages fail at TRACE to I_S.

C5 supplies worked-case evidence and demonstrates the method's discriminatory use, but it does not independently establish C2's generality.

Disposition: retain as case result.

## Rival architectures

### Rival A: flat five-contribution list

Rejected as a semantic representation. It obscures type differences and invites the false inference that all five are independent results.

### Rival B: one giant contribution

"Fixed-target authority-transfer framework" could absorb C1-C5, but destructive compression loses the distinction between method, application result, conditional boundary, and disciplinary implication.

Rejected for manuscript control, although usable as a one-sentence umbrella claim.

### Rival C: three-layer architecture

1. Core method: C1 + C2.
2. Results and limits: C5 + conditional C3.
3. Derived significance: C4.

This is the strongest reader-facing compression currently found. It preserves all five jobs while avoiding a false flat ontology.

Disposition: candidate preferred representation for contribution statements and manuscript planning. Preserve C1-C5 identifiers as subcomponents for traceability.

## Independent challenge

Strongest rival: collapse C1 into C2 and C3 into a limitation paragraph, yielding one method contribution plus case result and implication.

Challenge result: partial failure. C1 cannot be silently collapsed because the fixed-target tertium comparationis is what licenses comparison of heterogeneous native mechanisms. C3 can plausibly be compressed in manuscript presentation, but its semantic job cannot be deleted because multiple successful conflicting grounds create a problem not solved by route-level C2.

Strongest falsifier for C4: if target-relative authority merely restates the TRACE requirement, C4 adds no independent scholarly significance.

Result: OPEN at publication-placement level, not at semantic-content level. C4 is derivable and nonredundant as a disciplinary characterization, but whether it deserves a standalone Section 7 remains manuscript- and literature-sensitive.
