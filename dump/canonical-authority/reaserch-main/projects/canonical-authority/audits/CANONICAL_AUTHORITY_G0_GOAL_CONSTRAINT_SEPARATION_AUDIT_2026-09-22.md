## 1. Current G0

Current authoritative wording:

> Produce a rigorous source-grounded peer-reviewed paper using the fixed Rashi-Ramban
> disagreement on Genesis 1:1 to determine what selected second-order Jewish epistemic,
> interpretive, institutional, transmission, authority, and privileged-access structures
> can legitimately contribute to assessing incompatible truth-apt canonical claims.

## 2. Target decomposition

The sentence contains several different semantic jobs.

### A. Substantive inquiry target

Determine what epistemic grounds, if any, are available within Jewish thought for
truth-directed assessment of incompatible truth-apt canonical claims when shared
canonical standing alone does not adjudicate between them.

This is the candidate core research target.

### B. Deliverable / dissemination objective

Produce a rigorous peer-reviewed paper.

This specifies a desired output and publication state. It does not identify the
substantive question whose answer makes the paper successful.

### C. Fixed-case implementation

Use the Rashi-Ramban disagreement on Genesis 1:1.

This is the controlled worked case and a protected current project invariant. It makes
the inquiry inspectable and proposition-specific, but it is not itself the epistemic
target.

### D. Method / scope controls

Use selected second-order Jewish epistemic, interpretive, institutional,
transmission, authority, and privileged-access structures.

This specifies the present comparison domain and method of inquiry. The selected
module inventory is purposive and non-exhaustive.

### E. Quality / evidence controls

"rigorous" and "source-grounded" regulate admissibility and quality of the work.

They constrain acceptable execution rather than define the research question.

## 3. Provenance audit

Recovered project history distinguishes the substantive aim from its implementation.

Earlier project states repeatedly center on:
- whether shared canonical/traditional standing can determine which of two
  incompatible factual claims is true;
- what warrants preferring one incompatible canonical factual claim over another;
- the requirement for a further non-circular truth-discriminating basis;
- explanation of disagreement versus adjudication of truth.

The Rashi-Ramban case is repeatedly treated as the fixed primary case.

Publication is also a durable commitment, but it appears as an output commitment
alongside the substantive epistemic inquiry rather than as the content of that inquiry.

The September 19-21 reformulation adds the second-order source-module architecture,
typed source-to-target transfer, proposition-specific discrimination, evaluator
recognition, package TRACE, and publication controls. Those additions strengthen the
execution architecture without replacing the earlier truth-directed target.

## 4. HM-001 target-purity audit

Question:
What result becomes rationally available when the current G0 is achieved?

The current wording admits several non-equivalent answers:
- a paper exists;
- the Rashi-Ramban case has been analyzed;
- selected Jewish structures have been reconstructed;
- a truth-directed epistemic conclusion has been established.

These are not the same target state.

Defect:
TARGET_BUNDLING.

The authoritative target currently combines end state, output artifact, worked case,
method/scope, and quality constraints.

### Remove "rigorous source-grounded"

Classification:

### Remove "using the fixed Rashi-Ramban disagreement on Genesis 1:1"

The general substantive question remains intelligible and researchable, although the

Classification:
PROTECTED CASE CONSTRAINT / IMPLEMENTATION INVARIANT.

### Remove "selected second-order Jewish epistemic, interpretive, institutional,
transmission, authority, and privileged-access structures"

The central canonical-disagreement problem remains, but the current comparison method

### Remove the truth-directed question concerning incompatible canonical claims

The remaining sentence describes production of a paper about a case and some source
families but no longer states what the research is trying to learn.

Classification:
CORE TARGET.

Result:
The truth-directed canonical-disagreement question is the only component whose removal
destroys the substantive project aim.

## 6. Rival-target audit

### Rival A: publication target

"Produce a peer-reviewed paper."

Failure:
This permits success with a paper that fails to answer the substantive epistemic
question. It also makes external review/publication outcome part of the research
target even though that outcome is partly outside project control.

Rejected as the substantive goal.
Retained as a separate output / dissemination objective.

### Rival B: case target

"Determine what resolves the Rashi-Ramban Genesis 1:1 disagreement."

Failure:
This collapses the project into first-order case adjudication and contradicts the
durable boundary that the ultimate grammatical verdict can remain open.

Rejected.

### Rival C: method-defined target

"Determine what the currently selected seven source-module families contribute."

Failure:
This makes the present corpus constitutive of the goal and creates a hidden selector:
changing the module sample changes the goal rather than the evidence used to answer it.

Rejected as the top-level substantive goal.
Retained as current method/scope.

### Rival D: substantive epistemic target

"Determine what epistemic grounds, if any, are available within Jewish thought for
truth-directed assessment of incompatible truth-apt canonical claims when shared
canonical standing alone does not adjudicate between them."

Result:
This preserves the recurring historical inquiry, survives changes in publication
format, source-module sample, notation, and exact execution method, and still licenses
the current Rashi-Ramban case and second-order architecture.

Survives as the strongest candidate substantive goal.

## 7. Referent / representation audit

The project has used several labels:
- Mesorah;
- Canonical Factual Disagreement in Jewish Thought;
- Canonical Authority.

The durable referent is not identical to any one historical label.

The stable referent is the truth-directed problem created by incompatible canonical
claims whose shared canonical standing does not itself select the true proposition.

The later "authority" architecture is a refinement of how candidate grounds are typed
and transferred to that target.

Classification:
SAME_REFERENT_WITH_SCOPE_AND_METHOD_REFINEMENT, not a wholly new research aim.

## 8. Result-sensitivity audit

Tested coordinates:

### Publication format / venue

Changing journal or article format does not change the substantive answer.

Invariant for substantive goal.
Sensitive for dissemination objective.

### Fixed case

Changing the worked case can alter evidential demonstration and project identity but
does not alter the abstract substantive question.

Constraint-sensitive, not target-defining.

### Source-module inventory

Changing the admitted purposive sample can change the bounded answer and the strength
of broader claims.

Evidence/result-sensitive constraint.
Not target-defining.

### First-order grammatical verdict

The substantive second-order inquiry can succeed without deciding which reading of
Genesis 1:1 is grammatically correct.

Explicitly non-constitutive.

### Truth-directed success criterion

Changing from proposition-specific truth-directed grounds to legitimacy, authority,
provenance, explanation, or social recognition changes the question itself.

Target-defining.

### Jewish-thought domain

Removing the Jewish-thought domain broadens the project into a general philosophy or
religious-studies problem.

Target-scope defining.

## 9. Success-condition audit

A pure substantive goal cannot be closed merely by:
- completing a manuscript;
- analyzing all current modules;
- obtaining publication;
- reproducing the current bounded negative result.

Candidate substantive success condition:

Establish the strongest source-grounded answer warranted by the admitted evidence to
the question of what epistemic grounds, if any, are available within Jewish thought
for truth-directed assessment of incompatible canonical claims, with the scope of that
answer explicitly bounded by the evidence and comparison domain actually examined.

This success condition permits:
- a positive route;
- a negative bounded result;
- a mixed result;
- unresolved variation.

It does not preselect the current answer.

Separate dissemination success condition:

A rigorous manuscript accurately represents the established research result, closes
paper-facing source and novelty dependencies, survives internal challenge, and is
submitted for peer review under an appropriate venue-specific framing.

Publication acceptance remains an external terminal outcome rather than an internally
guaranteed completion state.

## 10. Goal versus constraints

### Candidate substantive goal

Determine what epistemic grounds, if any, are available within Jewish thought for
truth-directed assessment of incompatible truth-apt canonical claims when shared
canonical standing alone does not adjudicate between them.

### Output / dissemination objective

Develop and submit a rigorous peer-reviewed article that presents and defends the
result.

### Protected current constraints and invariants

- Use the Rashi-Ramban Genesis 1:1 disagreement as the fixed primary worked case.
- Preserve the exact proposition-level contradiction while leaving the ultimate
  first-order grammatical verdict open.
- Work at the second-order level rather than using first-order philology to decide the
  case.
- Reconstruct source content, native domain, and native function before case
  application.
- Use source-grounded evidence with recoverable source identity and provenance.
- Treat the admitted response-family corpus as purposive and non-exhaustive.
- Require proposition-specific truth-directed asymmetry before classifying a route as
  adjudicative.
- Keep truth adjudication distinct from legitimacy, authority, provenance,
  authentication, explanation, institutional settlement, and social recognition.
- Preserve evaluator-relative warrant where RECOGNIZE is invoked.
- Preserve the package-TRACE / COMPOSE distinction.
- State only bounded conclusions licensed by the recovered evidence.
- Do not convert the current bounded negative result into a universal negative.
- Keep broad new theory frozen unless an explicit reopen trigger is met.

## 11. Structural consequence

The present G0 mixes at least three control objects:

1. substantive research goal;
2. execution constraints / invariants;
3. publication / dissemination objective.

That mixing caused the complete G0 dependency graph to absorb source closure,
manuscript production, journal framing, and external publication outcome into the same
target object as the epistemic question.

A cleaner architecture separates those objects while preserving all current protected
research results.

## 12. Proposed authoritative repair

This audit recommends a goal change, but does not apply one.

Proposed replacement for CANON:G0:

> Determine what epistemic grounds, if any, are available within Jewish thought for
> truth-directed assessment of incompatible truth-apt canonical claims when shared
> canonical standing alone does not adjudicate between them.

Proposed separate dissemination goal:

> Develop and submit a rigorous source-grounded peer-reviewed article that presents,
> supports, and appropriately bounds the result of CANON:G0.

Proposed separate constraints control:

Create or designate a non-goal canonical control object containing the protected
case, scope, method, evidence, bounded-claim, and theory-freeze constraints listed in
section 10.

The existing G1 hierarchy requires reclassification after approval because several
current G1 nodes mix research dependencies with publication-readiness dependencies.

## 14. Audit verdict

Verdict:
CURRENT_G0_FAILS_TARGET_PURITY.

The failure is architectural rather than substantive.

The repository has a strong argument and current research result, but the top-level
goal conflates:
- what the project is trying to learn;
- how the inquiry is constrained;
- what artifact communicates the result;
- and an external publication outcome.

The strongest repair is to separate:
SUBSTANTIVE GOAL
from
CONSTRAINTS / INVARIANTS
from
DISSEMINATION OBJECTIVE.

No authoritative goal change has been made by this audit.
