## U1 — Fixed proposition-level collision

Starting state:
The reader knows only that Rashi and Ramban differ.

Live problem:
Is this merely a difference in explanation, interpretation, or emphasis, or is there a proposition-level factual incompatibility?

Needed resource:
Exact formulation of I_S and the rivals P_R / P_N.

First use:
State the disagreement without resolving its ultimate first-order grammar.

Inference licensed:
The paper has a fixed truth-apt target with incompatible rivals.

Epistemic change:
The reader now knows exactly what would have to be adjudicated.

Search-space change:
Purely explanatory accounts remain relevant as context but cannot by themselves answer the paper's target question.

Next live problem:
What is the actual truth-directed question created by the disagreement?

## U2 — Third-party adjudication question

Starting state:
The reader accepts that the rivals are incompatible.

Live problem:
What would count as grounds for one rival being favored as true by someone evaluating the dispute later?

Needed resource:
Q as the third-party truth-directed adjudication question.

First use:
Separate explanation-of-disagreement from truth adjudication.

Inference licensed:
The paper needs a mechanism that bears on truth rather than merely on origin, legitimacy, practice, or explanation.

Epistemic change:
The reader can now distinguish the paper's target from several tempting neighboring questions.

Search-space change:
Arguments that solve only explanation, legitimacy, bindingness, or institutional settlement become identifiable as potentially non-adjudicative outputs.

Next live problem:
How can unlike Jewish structures be compared without treating them as equivalent kinds of authority?

## U3 — Common target and H[F]

Starting state:
The reader sees the need for a truth-directed comparison but has heterogeneous Jewish mechanisms in view.

Live problem:
How can pluralism, transmission, authority, privileged access, institutional process, and related structures be compared without flattening them?

Needed resource:
Fixed target plus purposive H[F] response space.

First use:
Reconstruct each mechanism in native context while holding the target constant.

Inference licensed:
The fixed proposition pair can function as a common tertium comparationis.

Epistemic change:
The reader can now understand why native-function preservation and cross-mechanism comparison are compatible.

Search-space change:
The relevant candidates are not "all Jewish authority" but the admitted, materially differentiated response structures.

Next live problem:
What exactly does accepting each road commit us to?

## U4 — Native road reconstruction

Starting state:
The reader sees the response space but not why its internal diversity matters.

Live problem:
Could the different roads simply be interchangeable ways of saying the same thing?

Needed resource:
Road identity, native domain/function, bearer/provenance, and route-specific commitments.

First use:
Grant each road its strongest defensible native achievement and state the commitments that come with accepting it.

Inference licensed:
A road cannot be transferred across domains without preserving what made the road work and the commitments it carries.

Epistemic change:
The reader sees why road-specific commitments are substantive rather than editorial detail.

Search-space change:
Potential transfer routes are partitioned by their actual native outputs and commitments.

Next live problem:
Does anything each road natively supplies actually reach the fixed truth target?

## U5 — TRACE

Starting state:
A road has a defined native achievement and known commitments.

Live problem:
Is there a licensed path from that native output to the fixed proposition?

Needed resource:
R and TRACE.

First use:
Track source-grounded content through native domain/function, licensed transfer, truth relevance, and proposition-specific linkage.

Inference licensed:
A conclusion reached by a bridge not licensed by the source cannot be attributed to the source alone.

Epistemic change:
The reader now has a test for source-to-target transfer rather than an intuitive appeal to authority.

Search-space change:
Routes with no target path are stopped at TRACE; possible paths become inspectable rather than implicit.

Next live problem:
Even when a target-directed relation exists, does it actually favor one rival?

## U6 — DISCRIMINATE

Starting state:
The reader accepts that some source-to-target relation could exist.

Live problem:
Does that relation distinguish P_R from P_N?

Needed resource:
Target-specific discrimination; diagnostic T/L/I structure.

First use:
Test truth relevance, proposition-specific linkage, and asymmetric instantiation as one target-specific discriminatory relation.

Inference licensed:
Relevance to the truth target is not the same as a discriminator between rivals.

Epistemic change:
The reader can distinguish "bears on the question" from "selects between the answers."

Search-space change:
Symmetric or non-rival-specific considerations are removed from the set of case-selecting grounds.

Next live problem:
Even a real discriminator might not yet be usable by the evaluator.

## U7 — RECOGNIZE

Starting state:
The reader sees what a genuine discriminator would be.

Live problem:
Does evaluator e have warranted grounds to identify, authenticate, scope, and rely on it?

Needed resource:
W and RECOGNIZE.

First use:
Separate source-side discriminatory force from evaluator-relative uptake.

Inference licensed:
A truth-relevant discriminator can exist without being warrantedly usable by the evaluator in the case.

Epistemic change:
The reader can now distinguish source-relative support from evaluator-usable grounds.

Search-space change:
Private, inaccessible, unauthenticated, or otherwise unusable selectors are no longer treated as automatically adjudicative.

Next live problem:
Does the framework wrongly guarantee that no traditional mechanism could ever succeed?

## U8 — Positive-control safeguard

Starting state:
The reader has now seen increasingly demanding gates and may infer that religious adjudication has been ruled out in principle.

Live problem:
Is the method secretly constructed so that traditional mechanisms can never pass?

Needed resource:
Positive control: authenticated privileged access, with target coverage, discrimination, and evaluator warrant as hypothetical success conditions.

First use:
Show the architecture permits success in principle.

Inference licensed:
The paper's current negative/bounded result is empirical and corpus-relative, not definitional.

Epistemic change:
The reader understands the difference between "this corpus does not complete the route" and "no religious route could ever complete it."

Search-space change:
Hypothetically successful routes remain admissible in the general method, while the current case stays bounded.

Next live problem:
What does the current evidence actually establish?

## U9 — Case stopping point and bounded result

Starting state:
The reader has the complete route criteria and knows they are not impossibility conditions.

Live problem:
Where does the actual seven-module case stop?

Needed resource:
Current module results and package TRACE finding.

First use:
Apply the route method across the admitted corpus and identify the missing proposition-specific edge to I_S.

Inference licensed:
No currently admissible source package completes TRACE to I_S.

Epistemic change:
The reader now knows the strongest current case result and why downstream gates are not activated.

Search-space change:
All downstream selector/composition questions are demoted from current-case tasks to general-method or reopen conditions.

Next live problem:
What does the method say beyond this bounded case result?

## U10 — Conditional COMPOSE/defeat boundary

Starting state:
The reader knows the current case stops upstream.

Live problem:
Would multiple successful routes automatically produce a final case verdict if such routes existed?

Needed resource:
COMPOSE/defeat and admissible composition-set logic.

First use:
Distinguish package TRACE from aggregation of several already-completed grounds.

Inference licensed:
Route-level success does not itself entail all-things-considered case selection.

Epistemic change:
The reader understands the downstream boundary of the general method without confusing it with the present case result.

Search-space change:
Unlicensed composition rules are excluded from being silently attributed to the source routes.

Next live problem:
What broader implication follows from this entire structure?

## U11 — Target-relative and attribution-sensitive authority

Starting state:
The reader has seen the fixed case and the generalized transfer architecture.

Live problem:
What does the method imply about "authority" beyond this single disagreement?

Needed resource:
Target-relative authority and selector-attribution result.

First use:
Hold native success fixed and distinguish native achievement from proposition-specific truth transfer.

Inference licensed:
Authority is not epistemically portable by status alone; added bridge or selector machinery must be separately licensed and attributed.

Epistemic change:
The reader can generalize the result without flattening distinct religious authority structures.

Search-space change:
Generic authority claims split into native-function, transfer, discrimination, evaluator-uptake, and composition questions.

Next live problem:
What exactly is the paper's contribution?

## U12 — Contribution and disciplinary payoff

Starting state:
The reader has the case result, method, and broader implication.

Live problem:
What has this paper actually added relative to the literature?

Needed resource:
Tiered contribution map plus bounded novelty positioning.

First use:
State the fixed-target comparative method, route architecture, attribution/composition distinctions, and bounded case result in the order earned by the argument.

Inference licensed:
The paper can make a bounded intersection claim without claiming novelty for every component individually.

Epistemic change:
The reader can distinguish the paper's central contribution from supporting, conditional, and deferred material.

Search-space change:
Only contribution claims with an actual argument/literature pathway enter the manuscript.

## Reader-transition findings

### Finding 1 — U8 must be visible before U9

The positive control is not decorative validation. It prevents a predictable reader inference that the method was constructed so traditional adjudication cannot succeed.

Therefore U8 is best treated as a short guardrail immediately before or tightly integrated with the case application and bounded result.

### Finding 2 — U10 belongs after U9

COMPOSE is not part of the realized fixed-case path. Introducing it as a full stage before the case result risks making the reader think the case itself traversed a downstream gate it never reached.

Use U10 as a compact general-method boundary after the case stops.

### Finding 3 — U11 must follow U9/U10

The broader authority claim is derived from the worked case and route architecture. It should not appear as a detached thesis before the reader has watched the mechanism operate.

### Finding 4 — U12 is the earned endpoint

Contribution is an output of the completed argument, not an input to it.

The reader's final question becomes "what did this establish?" only after the prior units have done their work.
