## Observation codes

A01 — The abstract states a fixed Rashi-Ramban case but does not state the exact rival
propositions I_S / not-I_S. The phrase "Rashi-Ramban disagreement over Genesis 1:1"
therefore carries more specificity in the project than it does inside the abstract itself.

A02 — The corpus is called "current purposive, non-exhaustive seven-module corpus" but
the admission principle is not represented. The abstract states scope boundedness without
showing what makes the seven modules purposively related rather than merely selected.

A03 — The method presents TRACE, DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE clearly, but PROFILE is
described only in prose as "first reconstructs each admitted structure in context."
The distinction PROFILE-as-setup versus adjudicative gates is present implicitly rather
than explicitly.

A04 — "Independently admissible source package" appears before the abstract gives any
semantics for package admissibility. A reader can understand that package use is controlled,
but not what independence/admissibility consists in.

A05 — Authenticated prophecy is described as showing "traditional truth-directed success
is possible in principle." The abstract does not explicitly state the counterfactual
conditions: target coverage of I_S, rival discrimination, and warranted evaluator
authentication. The project contains those conditions; the abstract compresses them.

A06 — The case result is structurally precise about stopping at TRACE, but the phrase
"exact disputed proposition" remains underspecified because A01 omitted the exact proposition.

A07 — The final two sentences compress three different output types:
bounded case result;
derived methodological/disciplinary claim about target-relative authority;
conditional general-method requirement for composition/defeat.
Their dependency relations are not explicit inside the abstract.

A08 — COMPOSE appears only at the end, after the broader authority implication.
This is rhetorically compact but semantically non-linear: composition is a conditional
downstream method boundary, not a consequence of the broader authority claim.

A09 — The abstract contains no explicit novelty/comparator claim.
This is not automatically a defect in observation mode; it is an absent coordinate.

A10 — The abstract contains no explicit statement that the first-order grammatical verdict
is outside scope. It says the case is a "worked case" and focuses on grounds, but the exact
second-order boundary is not directly stated.

A11 — The abstract does not distinguish the project-level corpus admission operation from
package-level admissibility. Both are compressed into "each admitted structure" and
"independently admissible source package."

A12 — The abstract is information-dense but has a coherent six-move internal shape:
problem -> comparison/method -> three gates -> positive control -> bounded case result ->
broader implication + conditional composition.
The main compression pressure occurs in the last two moves, not uniformly throughout.

A13 — Source provenance and evidence identity are absent by design at this resolution.
The abstract cannot independently support source-verification, historical, or edition claims;
it can only state their outputs.

A14 — Reader-facing attribution is strong: bridge/composition machinery is explicitly
distinguished from source content. This survives compression unusually well.

A15 — Evaluator relativity is present in ordinary language ("third-party evaluator") and
in RECOGNIZE, even though the evaluator index e is not formalized.
