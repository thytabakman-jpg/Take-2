## Stage 2 — Decode the syntactic reversal

Primary Ramban:
Ramban explicitly says the secret of "bereshit" and the fact that Scripture did not say "Elohim bara bereshit" disclose the way of truth; bereshit hints to Hokhmah/Wisdom.

Independent Kabbalistic decoding evidence:
Later Kabbalistic exegesis explicitly reads the structure as Hokhmah/bereshit participating in the emanation/creation of Elohim/Binah. A scholarly discussion of the Pritzker Zohar translation likewise describes the mystical reading as reversing the ordinary grammatical relation: Ein Sof is implicit subject and Elohim is object.

Result:
Funkenstein's "Elohim as object" claim is structurally intelligible. It is not a loose metaphor. The esoteric parse can change the grammatical bearer roles of the verse.

Effect on I_S:
I_S is specifically a peshat proposition in which bara Elohim forms the main predication with Elohim as subject.
The sod reading described by Funkenstein is therefore not I_S.
