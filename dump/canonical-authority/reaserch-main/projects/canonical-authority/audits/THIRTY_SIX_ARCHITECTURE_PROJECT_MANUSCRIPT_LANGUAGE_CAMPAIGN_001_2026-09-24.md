## 0. Core correction

The discovered architecture is not 36 independent dimensions.

Let:

S = {SYS, SUB, COMP, INT, BND, XL}.

The full positional architecture is:

H = S x S.

Each cell s_i -> s_j asks how a result at one scope can legitimately affect another scope.

This matters for writing because local wording judgments cannot be promoted automatically.

Examples:

COMP -> SYS:
a better word does not imply a better title unless whole-title meaning, cadence, and reader expectation improve.

INT -> COMP:
a cadence or ambiguity problem can force a lexical repair.

BND -> SYS:
moving the main-title/subtitle boundary can change the title's identity and emphasis.

XL -> SYS:
journal/search/reader evidence can constrain the full title, but cannot silently change the paper's thesis.

SYS -> COMP:
the whole-paper goal can constrain which words are admissible.

## 5. Existing prose language audit

Sections 1-2 were tested recursively at:

paper -> section -> paragraph -> sentence/phrase.

Material findings:

L1:
Section 1 repeatedly says "the paper" where direct declarative prose is stronger.

L2:
several sentences use repository-method vocabulary before the reader needs it:
"controlled problem",
"under controlled conditions",
"comparison space".

L3:
Section 2 contains useful but slightly repetitive disclaimers around heterogeneity and boundedness.

L4:
the transition into Section 3 is strong and should be preserved.

L5:
mathematical symbols I_S/P_R/P_N are useful landmarks but should be visually/formally integrated later in typesetting rather than repeated excessively in prose.

Improvement Core language policy:

- prefer direct claims over metadiscourse;
- use "paper" only when describing scope/method rather than advancing the argument;
- preserve technical terms when they name actual method objects;
- remove repeated caveats after the relevant boundary has been established once;
- treat cadence/interface defects as legitimate causes of lexical/component repair;
- never promote a local wording improvement to section/paper level without upward-gated verification.
