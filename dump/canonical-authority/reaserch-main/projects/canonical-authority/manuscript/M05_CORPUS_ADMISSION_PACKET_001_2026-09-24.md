The reader must leave this movement understanding two things at once:

1. the seven admitted families were selected for analytically distinct jobs relevant to

   prove that no unexamined Jewish structure could contribute.

## Manuscript-ready admission principle

The paper does not attempt to catalogue every Jewish statement about authority,
tradition, disagreement, or interpretation. It admits a response family when the
family contributes a materially distinct second-order mechanism that could bear on the
fixed comparison, or when it serves an explicit control or boundary function.

Material distinctness is assessed through such coordinates as native domain, native
function, output type, proposition-relevant bridge, evaluator or institutional access,
and case-inferential role. Mere topical difference is not enough.

### H_M — protected reception

Distinct job:
tests whether provenance as received Mosaic content rather than reason-derived

Manuscript use:

### H_A — authority and error

### H_X — authenticated prophecy

### H_R — Ramban's creation tradition

Distinct job:

### H_L — loss and reconstruction

Distinct job:
tests whether a structure explaining forgotten and reconstructed Torah knowledge supplies

Manuscript use:
separates explanation of epistemic history from current truth discrimination.

### H_G — generational hierarchy

Distinct job:
tests whether comparative stature, memory, or intellectual hierarchy provides a licensed
reliability relation to the exact proposition.

Manuscript use:
separates rank/status asymmetry from proposition-specific reliability.

## Coverage claim

The corpus is:

source-grounded;
bounded;
non-exhaustive.

The paper may conclude what these admitted and reviewed mechanism families contribute to
the fixed comparison.

The paper may not conclude, merely from their failure, that every possible unexamined
Jewish source structure would also fail.

## Critical distinction

Corpus admission and package admissibility are different operations.

Corpus admission asks:

Which mechanism families belong in the paper's declared comparison space, and why?

Package admissibility asks:

When several already-admitted source structures are used together to establish one target-
indexed ground, is their joint use independently licensed?

Therefore:

corpus admission != package admissibility.

The first defines the bounded research comparison space.
The second constrains package-level TRACE inside that space.
