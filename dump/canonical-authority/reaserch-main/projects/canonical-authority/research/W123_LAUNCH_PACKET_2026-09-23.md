## Semantic preflight

DRAFT_SAFE:
A workstream is DRAFT_SAFE when every unresolved coordinate capable of changing the current manuscript architecture is explicitly represented and either (a) resolved, or (b) carried as an OPEN whose alternative resolutions do not silently alter the section dependency, central method, fixed-case result, contribution hierarchy, or bounded novelty framing.

CLOSED:
No live admitted evidence/capability under the frozen basis leaves a result-sensitive coordinate unresolved.

DRAFT_SAFE_OPEN:
Unresolved, but its live alternatives are explicitly represented and do not require a hidden manuscript-architecture choice.

BLOCKING_OPEN:
At least one live alternative can materially change the manuscript architecture, central method, fixed-case result, contribution hierarchy, or novelty framing.

Source closure:
Publication-facing source identity, evidential role, and provenance are recoverable for the claim that uses the source. This is claim-relative, not a demand to normalize every historical source in the repository.

Novelty closure:
The paper's bounded novelty language is supported against the strongest retrieved nearest neighbors on the actual typed contribution coordinates. It is not a universal literature-completeness claim.

Ceiling raise:
A same-goal successor that demonstrates strict publication-relevant gain while preserving protected results and passing independent challenge/non-regression.

Article admission:
A separate decision from theory validity. A valid theory result enters this article only when its manuscript gain exceeds added complexity/attack surface under the frozen publication task.
