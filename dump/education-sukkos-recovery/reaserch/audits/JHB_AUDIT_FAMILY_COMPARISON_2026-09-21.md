### New recursive finding H1-FICK-001

The transition from the Jewish coordination operation to the Sukkos stage contains a hidden bridge.

If the holiday were replaced by a generic activity, something educationally important must be lost.

The Fick route currently establishes:

- a coordination operation;
- a shared build;
- an edible sukkah;

It does not yet state what Sukkos contributes to the operation that a generic shared model-building task would not.

Therefore the route currently supports:

coordination operation
-> collaborative enactment

more clearly than:

coordination operation
-> specifically Sukkos embodiment/deepening.

This is not the same defect as JHB-FICK-003.

JHB-FICK-003 asks whether the activity enacts selective coordination.

H1-FICK-001 asks why the holiday itself contributes indispensable educational surplus.

Candidate location:
FICK-E03 / Page 2-to-Page 3 hinge.

Status:
NEW CANDIDATE FINDING.

The artifact already distinguishes HOLIDAY_EMBODIES from ACTIVITY_ENACTS at the relation vocabulary level.

The recursive audit shows why the distinction matters dynamically.

A successful edible build can satisfy enactment while leaving holiday-specific surplus underdetermined.

## Lens 1 — information loss

Question:
What materially relevant information is lost by the current representation?

### New finding H2-INFO-001

JHB-INV-018 says current Sukkos edible-sukkah construction must perform conceptual work.

The typed route register explicitly includes an ACTIVITY_ENACTS edge for Fick:

FICK-E04.

It does not include corresponding ACTIVITY_ENACTS edges for Keva or Conservation.

Yet Section 21 specifies route-specific edible-sukkah observations for all three Sukkos booklets.

Therefore the typed graph loses a distinction that exists in the prose project state:

- holiday embodiment;
- physical edible-sukkah enactment.

For Keva and Conservation these are compressed or omitted in the edge representation.

Effect:

The human-readable project can preserve an activity requirement that the typed graph fails to represent.

Status:
NEW REPRESENTATION FINDING.

This is not evidence that the Keva or Conservation activity itself fails.

It is evidence that the current typed route representation is incomplete.

## Lens 2 — inverse problem

Question:
Can observed success uniquely identify the curriculum mechanism?

Result:

No.

The master control already states this explicitly.

Examples:

- Yom Kippur benchmark success cannot identify which architecture component caused learning;
- successful Fick coordination cannot establish that the Fick -> Jewish operation -> Sukkah mechanism caused it;
- a successful Keva plan cannot establish keva as the causal mechanism.

No new finding.

The lens confirms that empirical validation needs failure diagnostics and mechanism discrimination rather than outcome-only scoring.

## Lens 3 — coordinate-system audit

Represent the same live route problems under several coordinates:

### Physical-page coordinates

- Fick issue appears on Page 3 and Page 4.
- Keva issue appears mainly on Page 2.
- Conservation issue spans Pages 1–3.

### Functional coordinates

- Fick separates holiday embodiment from activity enactment.
- Keva localizes to response-selection/discernment before regularization.
- Conservation localizes to cross-domain preserved/new content.

### Typed-edge coordinates

- Fick maps to FICK-E03/E04/E05.
- Keva maps sharply to KEVA-E03.
- Conservation maps to CONS-E03/E04/E05.

### Evidence coordinates

- Yom Kippur source-scope depends on exact accepted artifact identity.
- Fick repair authority depends on exact baseline identity.
- Conservation operation mode depends on baseline existence/identity.

Result:

The same substantive problem can look broad in page coordinates and narrow in relation coordinates.

This confirms that page-level repair alone is not a safe default.

## Lens 4 — selection effects

Question:
What did the project select as evidence for its shared architecture?

Result:

The project correctly limits its claims.

The Yom Kippur artifact shows that the four-page form can work.

It does not establish that the form is universally optimal.

The current architecture also derives from a small family of science-heavy booklets and subsequent repairs.

The master control explicitly bounds generalization beyond this series.

No immediate repair follows.

Watch condition:

Do not promote house architecture into general curriculum theory merely because it survives this four-booklet family.

## Lens 5 — counterfactual history / substitution

### Fick substitution test

Replace the sukkah build with a generic shared model-building task under common criteria.

Much of the selective-coordination lesson still survives.

Therefore the current record does not yet demonstrate what specifically Sukkos adds to the lesson.

This independently reproduces H1-FICK-001.

### Keva substitution test

Replace sukkah keva / house arai with a generic habit-building activity.

The source-grounded embodiment of regular dwelling is lost.

Therefore the current Keva Page 3 has a clearer holiday-specific surplus.

### Conservation substitution test

Replace the sukkah with a generic semi-open shelter.

A large portion of the bounded-but-not-sealed physical demonstration can survive.

This does not prove the Sukkos hinge fails because halakhic sukkah may supply additional constitutive structure.

But it strengthens JHB-CONS-001:

the control must make explicit what specifically Sukkos contributes beyond a generic semi-open enclosure.

## H2 synthesis

New findings:

1. H2-INFO-001 — typed route graph omits explicit ACTIVITY_ENACTS relations for Keva and Conservation.
2. Independent confirmation of H1-FICK-001 through counterfactual substitution.
3. Strengthening of JHB-CONS-001: holiday-specific surplus must be typed, not assumed from the presence of a sukkah.

Unique methodological yield:

H2 detects representation loss and counterfactual substitutability without following the project's historical discovery path.
