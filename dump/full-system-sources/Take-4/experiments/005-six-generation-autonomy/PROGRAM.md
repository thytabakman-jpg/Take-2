# ICC Six-Generation Autonomous Research Program

Date: 2026-09-24
Authority: experimental only
Owner: ICC
Scope: Take-4 experiments plus evidence-backed updates to research records. No promotion to production authority.

## ICC rewrite of the user's instruction

I own a six-generation autonomous research program over the complete M0-M11 mathematics map and the executable-controller problem.

I will not treat the six generations as six repetitions. Each generation inherits the previous generation's artifacts, evidence, failures, OPEN coordinates and protected behavior. It must reassess the live frontier before acting. It may revise the remaining plan when evidence defeats an assumption.

Every generation performs:

RECONSTRUCT -> CHOOSE -> ACT -> VERIFY -> UPDATE -> ASK "WHY?" -> ASK "WHAT'S NEXT?" -> HAND OFF

The next generation receives the full resulting state, not merely the previous conclusion.

I may create experiments, code, tests, mathematical artifacts and revisions inside experimental authority. I may not promote myself, alter production authority, erase OPEN/INCOMPARABLE, or call semantic evidence runtime evidence.

## Governing target

Turn the M0-M11 map from a collection of locally developed mathematics into an evidence-backed closed-loop controller architecture whose mathematical distinctions are demonstrated where possible and explicitly OPEN where not.

## Generation 1 — Runtime witness

Goal: establish whether the minimal closed loop actually executes.

Work:
1. execute Experiment 004;
2. capture worker acknowledgement, post-state observation, verification, re-entry and termination;
3. classify every failure by map coordinate rather than patch blindly;
4. update the frontier.

Exit evidence:
runtime PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED with trace.

## Generation 2 — Ablation and necessity

Goal: learn which closed-loop distinctions are load-bearing.

Work:
remove or perturb one coordinate at a time:
authority lock, observation, verification, reselection, hidden-constraint recovery, provenance.
Test whether protected behavior changes.

Questions:
Does M11 earn independent status?
Does explicit E or B add behavior beyond Q/T/F?
Which receipt fields are necessary?

Exit evidence:
ablation matrix with PASS/FAIL/OPEN and revised map dependencies.

## Generation 3 — Generalization

Goal: attack fixture overfitting.

Work:
construct multiple materially different fixtures, including wrong-object recovery, authority conflict, binding failure, evidence-induced reframe, incomparable continuations and no-gain termination.
Use one common controller policy schema where possible.

Exit evidence:
common-policy success boundary and counterexamples.

## Generation 4 — Mathematical reconstruction

Goal: use empirical results to rewrite the mathematics rather than force observations into old categories.

Work:
reassess M0-M11;
test the <Q,T,F | E,B> hypothesis;
formalize any demonstrated feedback relations, closure conditions, capability quantifiers, observation sufficiency and termination conditions;
retain irreducible OPEN coordinates.

Exit evidence:
revised dependency graph plus theorem/conjecture/counterexample set.

## Generation 5 — Architecture challenge

Goal: test whether the mathematics supports a smaller or stronger controller/kernel architecture.

Work:
compare incumbent Take-4 experimental architecture against at least one rival reconstruction;
test behavioral preservation and strict gain;
distinguish capability existence, reachability and controller realizability;
test whether placement above/inside kernel is behaviorally relevant under the protected quotient.

Exit evidence:
nondominated architecture frontier. No forced winner.

## Generation 6 — Synthesis and autonomous continuation

Goal: integrate everything learned and decide what the program has actually established.

Work:
audit all six generations for contradiction, unsupported promotion, regression and unresolved cuts;
update canonical experimental math records where evidence licenses it;
state verified results, synthetic-only results, rejected hypotheses, OPEN problems and capability boundaries;
ask Why?
ask What's next?
choose the next autonomous research program or terminate with a goal-relative fixed point.

## Reassessment rule

The numbered generations are a scaffold, not a command to continue a falsified plan.
After each generation ICC reconstructs the frontier. It may change the remaining generation content when the evidence gives a concrete reason. It preserves the original plan and records the revision rather than rewriting history.

## Handoff object

Each generation hands forward:
goal;
protected behavior;
artifacts;
observations;
verified results;
failed claims;
OPEN/incomparable coordinates;
authority state;
current frontier;
its answer to Why?;
its answer to What's next?.

## Stop conditions

ICC stops or pauses only at:
verified goal-relative fixed point;
exactly identified external blocker;
required user authority;
unresolved OPEN with no licensed discriminator;
or demonstrated no-gain continuation.

No generation may self-promote the controller.
