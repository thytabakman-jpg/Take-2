# Experiment 002 — Operator Mathematics Legacy × Take-2

Date: 2026-09-24
Status: PLANNED_CONTROLLED_REPLAY

## Why this experiment exists

The core-operator mathematics repair was completed in the legacy Reaserch repository,
not through the Take-2 kernel. Take-2 must not receive retrospective credit for work it
did not perform.

The legacy result is now a controlled reference case. Replaying the same job through
Take-2 tests whether the PD-centered kernel reproduces the material discoveries, finds
additional defects, improves traceability, or reduces operating-surface complexity.

## Frozen experimental question

Given the pre-repair operator mathematics as input, does the Take-2 PD-centered kernel:

1. independently recover the material defects and repairs;
2. find material defects the legacy run missed;
3. preserve OPEN, incomparability, partiality, and protected results;
4. provide a clearer reconstruction of why each state transition occurred;
5. require fewer dedicated durable operating surfaces;
6. avoid importing legacy conclusions merely because they are known to us?

## Anti-leakage rule

The legacy repaired result is the scoring/reference object, not the candidate generator
for the Take-2 replay. The replay must begin from the pre-repair mathematical artifact
and frozen job. Legacy answers are revealed only during comparison/A16 scoring.

## Take-2 loop

Observe
-> Freeze/Type
-> PD discovery and representation attack
-> select capabilities under evidence status
-> produce candidate formal successor(s)
-> hostile verification
-> Transition only under experiment authority
-> Observe results
-> compare against legacy reference
-> re-enter PD/RTC on material delta.

## Measurements

Report separately:
- legacy material findings independently recovered;
- new material findings;
- missed legacy findings;
- false/invalid findings;
- OPEN coordinates;
- incomparable successors preserved;
- number of durable surfaces touched;
- number of transitions;
- manual interventions;
- validator/fitness results;
- observable execution time where available;
- whether a dedicated new registry/surface was required;
- explanation trace from observation to accepted result.

Do not collapse these into one scalar score without a justified measurement model.

## Success condition

Take-2 earns evidence of value when it produces strict non-regressive gain on at least
one declared dimension while preserving the protected legacy semantics. Exact
reproduction alone demonstrates viability, not superiority.
