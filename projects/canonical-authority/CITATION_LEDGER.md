## Rule

Each external source receives one stable numeric ID. The same ID may support many manuscript claim units.

Do not renumber existing IDs when a source is added or removed. New sources take the next unused number.

Status values:

- **VERIFIED** — source inspected and the relevant proposition is supported.
- **VERIFIED / ANALYTIC** — source premise is verified; the manuscript's further inference is its own argument.
- **YELLOW** — substantially supported, but wording, edition, or locator needs publication-level tightening.
- **OPEN** — exact source or passage still needs to be acquired or inspected.
- **REFERENCE** — biographical/date or bibliographical authority rather than substantive source evidence.
- **PROJECT RECORD** — evidence about this project's own research process rather than an external historical claim.

The ledger records what a source can support. It does not authorize a stronger claim than the source actually establishes.

# A. Primary Jewish sources

## [1] Rashi on Bereishis 1:1

**Type:** PRIMARY  
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Locator:** Rashi on Genesis 1:1  
**Supports:**

- Rashi's peshat reads the opening as a dependent temporal construction leading to God's command for light.
- Rashi states the broad grammatical premise that scriptural reishis occurs in construct relation.
- Rashi's extant comment explicitly handles Yeshayahu 46:10 through ellipsis, supplying an omitted “thing.”
- The opening is not offered as the chronological first act of creation.

**Publication note:** Select and normalize a final critical/standard edition. Sefaria is adequate for current verification, not necessarily the final edition citation.

## [2] Ramban on Bereishis 1:1

**Type:** PRIMARY  
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Locator:** Ramban on Genesis 1:1, especially the grammatical discussion following his quotation of Rashi  
**Supports:**

- Ramban identifies Yeshayahu 46:10 as a non-construct reishis.
- Ramban argues that if an omitted davar is supplied there, an omitted kol can likewise be supplied in Bereishis 1:1.
- Ramban cites Devarim 33:21 as another non-construct case.
- Ramban advances his own plain-sense reading in which bereishis is not in Rashi's dependent relation.
- Ramban separately states that creation is a profound secret known through received tradition reaching Moshe from God.

**Important control:** [2] supports the logical relation of Ramban's surviving objection to extant Rashi. It does not establish that Ramban historically saw every sentence now present in extant Rashi. See [3].

## [3] Eliyahu Mizrahi / Re'em on Bereishis 1:1

**Type:** PRIMARY  
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Locator:** Mizrachi, Genesis 1:1, discussion of Ramban's objection to Rashi; Sefaria segmentation around 1:1:11  
**Supports:**

- This supports a textual-history explanation of why Ramban's presentation may not engage the extant Rashi sentence directly.
