# 1. The Disagreement and the Question

## 1.1 The Exact Disagreement

<!-- P01 -->
Canonical status does not eliminate factual disagreement. Genesis 1:1 offers a useful case because Rashi and Ramban do not merely emphasize different aspects of the verse. They advance incompatible claims about how its opening syntax works on the peshat they defend. Rashi reads the opening words as part of a dependent temporal construction. The verse, on this reading, does not begin with an independent assertion that God created the heavens and the earth. It supplies the temporal setting for the later predication, so that the sense runs through the description of the earth and reaches the divine speech that follows. Ramban rejects that construction as the peshat of the verse. He treats the opening as an independent creation statement and explains what that initial creation consisted in before turning to the subsequent formation of the world. The disagreement is therefore narrow enough to state precisely and strong enough to generate a genuine truth conflict.

<!-- P02 -->
Let (I_S) denote the proposition that, on the peshat advanced, Genesis 1:1 has *bara Elohim* as its main predication rather than as part of a temporal subordinate construction leading to verse 3. Rashi's position can then be represented as (P_R = \neg I_S), while Ramban's can be represented as (P_N = I_S). The notation does not decide which reading is correct. It fixes the object of disagreement. Under that description, the rival claims cannot both correctly characterize the same syntactic relation in the same sense. Accepting Rashi at this point excludes Ramban, and accepting Ramban excludes Rashi. That gives the paper a stable proposition pair against which different kinds of religious authority, transmission, interpretation, and privileged access can be tested.

## 1.2 The Question for a Later Evaluator

<!-- P03 -->
The question is not which reading this paper can establish by independent grammatical analysis. It is a second order question about grounds. Given two incompatible canonical claims, what source grounded basis, if any, can a later evaluator use to favor one of them as true? The point of asking the question this way is to isolate a problem that arises inside a tradition without requiring the scholar to settle the underlying theological or philological dispute. Rashi and Ramban can remain the first order claimants while the analysis asks what kinds of second order structures could legitimately bear on the truth directed comparison between them. The target remains fully truth apt even though the paper itself does not pronounce the final grammatical verdict.

<!-- P04 -->
Several nearby questions can look like answers while doing different work. A tradition can regard both views as legitimate, designate one as binding for practice, classify one teaching as received rather than inferred, authenticate a speaker, explain why a disagreement arose, or grant an institution authority to settle a dispute. Each of those outputs can matter. None is identical to showing that one of the two propositions is descriptively true. Canonicality itself has the same limitation. To call Rashi and Ramban canonical identifies an important status within Jewish intellectual life, but status alone does not yet supply a proposition specific relation favoring (P_R) over (P_N), or the reverse. The problem is therefore not whether authority, tradition, or institutional settlement matter. It is what epistemic work each mechanism actually performs, and whether that work can be connected to this particular truth dispute without importing a conclusion the source itself does not provide.

# 2. Fixing the Target and Defining the Comparison

## 2.1 One Fixed Target

<!-- P05 -->
The comparison proceeds by holding the disputed proposition fixed while allowing the mechanisms themselves to remain heterogeneous. A halakhic rule for handling disagreement, a doctrine about received Mosaic content, an institution with binding authority, a hierarchy of generations, and a procedure for authenticating prophecy need not be instances of one underlying kind of authority. Their common coordinate is not native function. It is the question put to them. What, if anything, can each contribute to the assessment of (P_R) and (P_N)? This form of fixed target comparison preserves difference rather than erasing it. The mechanisms are reconstructed in their own domains first, then tested against the same proposition pair. A common target therefore permits comparison without requiring functional identity.

## 2.2 What Counts as Success

<!-- P06 -->
A mechanism counts as progress only when its native output can be related to the fixed truth target in a licensed way. This requirement matters because native success and target success are not the same thing. A rule can successfully determine practice without establishing descriptive truth. A provenance classification can successfully identify a teaching as received without showing that the received material includes (I_S). An institution can successfully exercise authority while remaining fallible about a factual matter. An authentication procedure can establish that a source has privileged access without showing that the access covers this proposition. The analysis therefore asks for an explicit relation between what the mechanism natively establishes and what would be needed to distinguish the rival claims. The full route architecture will be introduced later. At this stage, the key point is simpler. The bridge from native function to target truth must be shown rather than assumed.

## 2.3 Why These Mechanisms

<!-- P07 -->
The corpus is purposive and non exhaustive. It includes seven mechanism families because they represent materially different second order possibilities for bearing on the fixed question. Pluralism and determination test whether legitimacy or practical settlement can function as a truth selector. Protected reception tests whether the distinction between received and reason derived content supplies an epistemic asymmetry. Authority and error test institutional force under acknowledged fallibility. Authenticated prophecy tests privileged epistemic access and serves as a positive control. Ramban's received creation tradition tests the route closest to the actual Genesis case. Loss and reconstruction test whether an account of forgotten and recovered knowledge supplies present adjudicative force. Generational hierarchy tests whether comparative stature, memory, or intellectual rank can be connected to reliability on this proposition. The point is not that these seven exhaust Jewish thought. It is that they create a deliberately varied comparison set capable of exposing several different ways a second order route can succeed, fail, or stop short.

<!-- P08 -->
A second selection problem appears later and must remain distinct from corpus admission. Corpus admission asks why a mechanism belongs in the comparison at all. Package admissibility asks whether two or more already admitted sources may legitimately be used together to establish one target indexed ground. The distinction matters because individually incomplete sources can sometimes become jointly informative. A method that tested only one source at a time would risk mistaking singleton failure for corpus failure. Allowing source packages repairs that problem without turning every possible combination into an admissible argument. The package itself still requires an independent basis for combining its members. This later question concerns how an admitted set can jointly support one ground, not whether new mechanisms have silently entered the corpus.

# 3. What the Sources Actually Do

## 3.1 Pluralism and Received Tradition

<!-- P09 -->
The pluralism and determination material illustrates why native reconstruction comes first. Eruvin 13b preserves the famous formulation that both rival positions can be "the words of the living God" while also recording a practical determination in favor of Beit Hillel. The Ritva, reporting an answer attributed to the Rabbanei Tzarfat, develops one way of understanding how opposed positions can possess standing within the divine legal order. Modern scholarship on rabbinic pluralism has shown that the meaning and scope of such materials remain contested, which itself counsels against treating "elu ve elu" as a ready made theory of factual truth. For the present paper, the narrower point is enough. These sources concern legitimacy within disagreement and, in the Talmudic passage, practical determination. A rule that preserves legitimacy or determines practice does not yet tell us whether Rashi or Ramban is descriptively correct about the syntax of Genesis 1:1. To do that work, an additional relation would have to connect the native function of the pluralism or determination rule to (I_S).

<!-- P10 -->
Maimonides supplies a different structure when he distinguishes received Mosaic material from matters derived through interpretation and legal reasoning. In Hilkhot Mamrim and in his Introduction to the Mishnah, provenance matters because not every element of Torah knowledge stands in the same relation to reception and dispute. This can be epistemically significant. A proposition known to belong to a protected received category could possess a status that a later inference does not. Yet that classification alone does not select between (P_R) and (P_N). The analysis would still need grounds for placing the disputed syntactic proposition within the protected received content, and for showing that the classification asymmetrically favors one rival. Protected reception can therefore classify the kind of knowledge at issue, or relocate a disagreement outside the protected category, without automatically adjudicating the proposition itself.

## 3.2 Authority, Loss, and Hierarchy

<!-- P11 -->
The authority and error materials separate institutional force from factual correctness even more sharply. The Horayot tradition treats authoritative rulings and error within the same legal framework. Mishnah Horayot, the Bavli, and Maimonides' codification can regulate what follows from an erroneous authoritative decision precisely because authority does not make error conceptually impossible. That structure matters for the present comparison. An institution can govern legal consequences or command compliance while still requiring a further argument before its authority becomes evidence that a descriptive proposition is true. Applied to the Genesis case, institutional standing would need a truth directed relation to (I_S) and an asymmetry between the rival readings. Without that relation, authority can settle one kind of problem while leaving the present one untouched.

<!-- P12 -->
Loss and reconstruction, and generational hierarchy, expose two other ways in which epistemically important claims can stop short of a present selector. Temurah 16a describes Torah material forgotten after Moses' death and a process of recovery through reasoning. That narrative can explain how transmission is interrupted and how knowledge can later be reconstructed, but explanation of epistemic history is not yet a rule for choosing between Rashi and Ramban. Likewise, statements in Shabbat 112b and Eruvin 53a express asymmetries of generational stature, memory, or intellectual capacity. Such asymmetries can matter without establishing a monotonic rule that earlier always means more accurate on every proposition type. Later halakhic sources can even recognize forms of later informational advantage. To use generational hierarchy here, the paper would need a source grounded relation between the relevant kind of stature and reliability on Biblical Hebrew syntax, together with a case specific asymmetry between the two commentators.

## 3.3 Prophecy and Ramban's Creation Tradition

<!-- P13 -->
Authenticated prophecy differs because its native function is much closer to truth directed privileged access. In Hilkhot Yesodei HaTorah, Maimonides both constrains the legal role of prophecy and sets out conditions by which a prophet is authenticated. The mechanism therefore does not merely confer social status. It concerns a purported channel of knowledge whose credibility can, under the right conditions, be assessed. This makes prophecy an important positive control for the paper's method. An authenticated prophet whose revelation actually covered (I_S), selected one of the rival propositions, and was recognizable to the evaluator through warranted authentication would provide the kind of truth directed route the analysis is looking for. Nothing in the current case establishes those conditions. The importance of the example is structural. Traditional religious mechanisms are not excluded from success by definition.

<!-- P14 -->
Ramban's own discussion of creation is more case proximate. In his comment on Genesis 1:1, he explicitly treats the process of creation as a profound matter known fully through Mosaic tradition. Yet the same comment also contains a separately articulated peshat discussion and later introduces an esoteric "way of truth." The current source record therefore establishes that received creation knowledge and the interpretation of Genesis 1 coexist within Ramban's treatment. It does not establish that the syntactic proposition (I_S) is itself one of the received esoteric contents. The distinction is not pedantic. Topic proximity is weaker than proposition coverage. A tradition can concern creation without containing every exegetical judgment made in the same discussion. The strongest case proximate route therefore reaches the right topic and the right commentator, but the proposition specific edge remains unestablished.

## 3.4 Where the Sources End and the Analysis Begins

<!-- P15 -->
This distinction fixes the boundary between source reconstruction and project level application. A source can supply a bearer, native domain, native function, provenance claim, authority relation, or authentication structure. The analysis can then ask whether that material supports a bridge to (I_S). But information introduced only by that bridge cannot be credited retroactively to the source. The same rule applies to later combination. When an added principle determines how two source outputs interact, the result may be legitimate as an augmented argument, but its selecting information belongs partly to the added principle. This attribution discipline allows the paper to use philosophical analysis without confusing analysis with recovered source content. It also prepares the central methodological question. Once each mechanism has been reconstructed in its own terms, what has to happen before any of it becomes a ground for preferring (P_R) or (P_N)?

# 4. From Source to Ground

## 4.1 TRACE

<!-- P16 -->
The first task is TRACE. PROFILE names the prior setup work already performed in the source reconstructions. It identifies what the source is, what it natively does, and which commitments travel with it. TRACE then asks a new question. Does an independently admissible source package supply a licensed and source attributable relation from that native material to the fixed target? A source need not use the language of modern epistemology, and it need not have been written to answer the Rashi Ramban dispute. What matters is whether the proposed application can be reconstructed without inserting the very proposition specific force that the application is meant to demonstrate. TRACE therefore turns a loose appeal to "authority" into an inspectable path from source content to (I_S).

<!-- P17 -->
TRACE must be package aware. Early versions of the analysis risked treating failure of every singleton route as failure of the corpus, but that inference is not valid in general. Two incomplete sources can sometimes jointly establish what neither establishes alone. The repaired method therefore allows an independently licensed package (X) to be the bearer of one target indexed ground, with a one source package as the special singleton case. The license matters. The method does not permit arbitrary source aggregation until some combination yields a desired result. It asks whether there is a principled basis for using the sources together and whether their combined content actually completes the target relation. This is still TRACE because the package is being used to establish one ground. It is not yet the later problem of choosing among several completed grounds.

## 4.2 DISCRIMINATE

<!-- P18 -->
Reaching the target is not enough. A route can be relevant to (I_S) without favoring either rival. DISCRIMINATE asks whether the traced relation is asymmetric in the fixed case. Does it support (P_R) over (P_N), or (P_N) over (P_R), concerning truth? This requirement prevents topic relevance from masquerading as adjudication. It also compresses several lower level diagnostics into one reader facing job. Proposition linkage, case instantiation, and truth relevance matter because together they must yield a rival specific difference. When they do not, the route may illuminate the dispute while still leaving the evaluator without a reason to choose between the competing claims.

## 4.3 RECOGNIZE

<!-- P19 -->
Even a genuine source side discriminator does not automatically become a usable ground for a later evaluator. RECOGNIZE marks that additional step. The evaluator needs warranted grounds for identifying the relevant source relation and relying on it in the case at hand. Contemporary work on expertise and epistemic authority helps clarify why source side support and evaluator uptake are distinct. A source can possess an epistemically important relation that a third party cannot reliably identify, authenticate, or use. In religious contexts, texts, institutions, interpretive communities, and recognized bearers can contribute to that uptake, but social recognition is not itself sufficient. It matters insofar as it participates in warranted access to the discriminator. The route level success condition is therefore layered. TRACE and DISCRIMINATE establish a source side ground; RECOGNIZE concerns its evaluator specific usability.

## 4.4 The COMPOSE Boundary

<!-- P20 -->
A further problem arises only when several completed grounds exist and materially conflict. At that point, success of each route does not yet determine the all things considered case result. COMPOSE names the separately licensed rules by which completed grounds can be aggregated, defeated, ranked, tied, or left unresolved. This must remain distinct from package TRACE. Package TRACE allows several sources to work together to establish one ground. COMPOSE operates only after several grounds already exist. The distinction also exposes a provenance issue. If the source side grounds are held fixed while a different composition rule changes the verdict, the selecting information is being introduced by the composition layer. A warranted conclusion can therefore be source only, mixed provenance, rule relative, tied, or unresolved. In the present Rashi Ramban case this downstream machinery remains conditional because the analysis never reaches multiple completed conflicting grounds.

# 5. Testing the Method

## 5.1 Positive Control

<!-- P21 -->
The prophetic route shows that the framework does not define religious authority out of epistemic relevance. Imagine an authenticated prophetic source whose revelation explicitly covered the syntactic proposition (I_S), whose content favored one of the rival readings, and whose prophetic status and target coverage were warrantedly recognizable to the evaluator. That route would TRACE to the proposition, DISCRIMINATE between the rivals, and satisfy the evaluator side condition for use. The example is deliberately conditional. Maimonides' account does not establish that such a revelation exists for Genesis 1:1. Its function in the argument is to demonstrate non vacuity. The method can register traditional privileged access as genuine truth directed force when the source itself supplies the required relations.

## 5.2 The Strongest Case Proximate Route

<!-- P22 -->
The actual case is harder because Ramban's creation tradition gets close without completing the decisive edge. Ramban unquestionably invokes received knowledge concerning creation. That fact gives the route a stronger initial claim to relevance than generic appeals to authority or stature. Yet the target is not "creation" as a topic. It is (I_S), a particular syntactic proposition in Ramban's peshat. The currently inspected primary source distinguishes the peshat discussion from the esoteric material strongly enough that co location cannot substitute for a proposition specific bridge. To complete TRACE, one would need evidence that the received creation knowledge contains (I_S), entails it, or otherwise supplies truth support that asymmetrically bears on it. The recovered source record does not presently provide that link.

<!-- P23 -->
The strongest retrieved scholarship does not erase the problem. David Berger, discussing Ramban's relation between peshat and Kabbalah, reports Amos Funkenstein's argument that Genesis 1:1 can display syntactic tension between the peshat and the "way of truth," with the grammatical role of *Elohim* differing across the two readings. David Shatz likewise reports scholarship that treats Ramban's Kabbalah as deeply involved in his reading of Genesis 1 while preserving the need to distinguish what exactly the mystical material contributes. These sources make any simple separation between exegesis and Kabbalah too crude, but they also make a simple identification of the peshat syntax with received esoteric content unsafe. Funkenstein's full text and the relevant portions of Moshe Halbertal's study remain open acquisition points in the current research record. The result must therefore remain calibrated to what has actually been inspected.

## 5.3 Where the Case Stops

<!-- P24 -->
Once source packages are tested rather than only singleton routes, the current bounded result survives. Across the seven reconstructed mechanism families, no independently admissible package presently supplies the missing proposition specific edge needed to complete TRACE to (I_S). Some routes establish legitimacy, provenance, institutional force, epistemic history, hierarchy, or authenticated privileged access in principle. Ramban's creation tradition comes closest to the case itself. None of the currently reconstructed packages, however, converts those materials into a source attributable ground that reaches the exact syntactic proposition. The realized case therefore stops at TRACE. Because DISCRIMINATE, RECOGNIZE, and COMPOSE lie downstream of that obstruction, changing evaluator assumptions or composition rules cannot alter this fixed case result while the TRACE gap remains. This is a corpus bounded and evidence bounded conclusion, not a universal claim about every possible Jewish source or every evidence set that could later be recovered.

# 6. Authority Across Targets

## 6.1 Why Authority Does Not Automatically Transfer

<!-- P25 -->
The comparison yields a broader implication about authority. Authority is not epistemically portable merely because the same bearer, institution, text, or tradition possesses genuine standing somewhere else. The source modules examined here succeed at different native jobs. Some legitimate disagreement. Some determine practice. Some classify provenance. Some govern institutional consequences. Some describe epistemic loss or hierarchy. Some authenticate privileged access. Their force with respect to those jobs can be real while their force with respect to (I_S) remains unestablished. Truth directed transfer therefore depends on the target. A mechanism that is authoritative for one output cannot simply carry that authority label across domains as a substitute for a relation to a new proposition. This is not a claim that religious authority lacks epistemic force. It is a claim about the conditions under which that force can be transported.

## 6.2 Where Selector Information Comes From

<!-- P26 -->
The same analysis also makes selector provenance visible. Suppose a source supplies content (S), but (S) does not distinguish the rival propositions until an interpreter adds bridge principle (B). If changing (B) while holding (S) fixed changes the verdict, then the verdict is not attributable to (S) alone. The additional information comes from the bridge. The same is true of a composition rule applied to several completed grounds. This does not make mixed provenance illicit. It makes attribution necessary. A conclusion can properly be described as arising from source content plus an explicitly licensed inferential rule, or from source content as usable by an evaluator under specified warrants. What the analysis excludes is a silent transfer in which a selector introduced by the analyst, community, or meta rule is redescribed as though it had always been contained in the underlying source.

## 6.3 What the Scholar Can Claim

<!-- P27 -->
This division of labor also locates the paper's disciplinary claim. The scholar need not decide whether Rashi or Ramban is ultimately correct in order to analyze which internal Jewish structures are presented as capable of bearing on truth, what those structures natively establish, and what additional relations are required before they can adjudicate this dispute. The first order target remains emic in the important sense that the rival claims concern what is true. The analysis is etic in the sense that it reconstructs and compares the inferential roles of the mechanisms without demanding assent to their theological premises. That combination permits rigorous truth directed analysis in religious studies without turning the article into a theological verdict. It also gives the paper a way to distinguish empirical source reconstruction, conceptual transfer analysis, and the final bounded case result rather than treating them as one evidential layer.

# 7. What the Analysis Establishes

## 7.1 The Contribution

<!-- P28 -->
The contribution is best understood as a dependency package rather than a list of independent novelties. First, the paper fixes one rival proposition pair and uses it to compare heterogeneous mechanisms without flattening their native functions. Second, it reconstructs the source to ground route as PROFILE, TRACE, DISCRIMINATE, and RECOGNIZE, with TRACE capable of operating over independently admissible source packages. Third, it distinguishes package TRACE from the later COMPOSE problem that arises only when several completed grounds conflict, while requiring outcome material selector information to be attributed. Fourth, it applies that architecture to the Rashi Ramban case and obtains a bounded package level TRACE obstruction, protected by a positive control showing that success is possible in principle. Finally, it derives a target relative and attribution sensitive account of authority transfer. Each later contribution depends on the earlier ones. None is merely announced at the end.

## 7.2 Novelty in the Reviewed Comparator Corpus

<!-- P29 -->
The paper's novelty claim is correspondingly narrow. The relevant neighboring literatures are not empty. Joshua Halberstam addresses epistemic disagreement and *elu ve elu*. Yuval Sinai and Martin Golding examine factual disagreement in Jewish law. Aviram Ravitsky analyzes halakhic truth and erroneous ruling. Alvin Goldman, Jan Constantin and Thomas Grundmann, and Linda Zagzebski provide important resources on expertise and epistemic authority. R. W. L. Moberly addresses prophecy and discernment. Oliver Freiberger develops comparative methodology for religious studies. Ari Kelman and rafa kern articulate a sociology of religious knowledge. These works occupy important parts of the surrounding space. In the comparator corpus reviewed for this project, however, no direct predecessor has been located for the combined architecture used here, namely fixed target comparison of heterogeneous Jewish mechanisms, source package TRACE, rival specific DISCRIMINATE, evaluator relative RECOGNIZE, a separate multi ground COMPOSE boundary, and explicit attribution of selector information. The novelty claim therefore belongs to the intersection, not to each component taken alone.

## 7.3 The Bounded Answer

<!-- P30 -->
The paper can now give a precise answer to the question with which it began. Across the currently admitted and audited second order structures, the analysis has not recovered an independently admissible source package that supplies a completed, proposition specific, third party usable ground for selecting between Rashi's (P_R) and Ramban's (P_N). That result does not establish that Jewish thought contains no such ground. The corpus is purposive rather than exhaustive, several publication facing source tasks remain open, and new evidence could reopen the strongest case proximate route. What the method adds is a way to state exactly what would have to change. A future source or source package would need to supply a licensed relation to (I_S), generate a rival specific asymmetry, and be warrantedly usable by the relevant evaluator. Until such an edge is established, canonical standing, native authority, provenance, explanation, and topic proximity cannot do the missing work by themselves. The boundedness of the conclusion is therefore not a retreat from the analysis. It is part of what makes the conclusion recoverable, falsifiable, and proportionate to the evidence.
