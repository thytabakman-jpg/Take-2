## 3. Earned claims that had been weakened and are now restored

### Restored 1 — shared epistemic weight

Strong current form:
Shared canonical standing can give both rivals epistemic weight while still failing to select between them because the evidential feature is shared.

### Restored 2 — universal-premise counterexample

Strong current form:
If Yeshayahu 46:10 or Devarim 33:21 is a genuine counterexample to Rashi's universal reishis premise, that premise is false as stated. This does not by itself establish Ramban's full reading of Bereishis 1:1.

### Restored 3 — falsehood under non-contradiction

Strong current form:
Under ordinary classical non-contradiction, preservation of P and not-P preserves at least one false proposition. If both are included in a broad mesorah category, the category so defined contains at least one false proposition.

Boundary:
This is conditional on the broad category and classical treatment. It is not the claim that every item in mesorah is false or that all meanings of mesorah include both rivals.

### Restored 4 — convergence

Strong current form:
Heterogeneous response routes carry different commitments and fail at different locations, but every successful truth-adjudicative route converges on a truth-relevant discriminator plus warranted uptake.

### Restored 5 — successful traditional discriminators count as success

Strong current form:
The method does not force traditional responses to fail. A genuine traditional transmission, authority, or privileged-access asymmetry succeeds when it meets the target, discrimination, and evaluator conditions.

### Restored 6 — falsifiability/revision rules

The paper now states explicit defeat/revision conditions rather than vague limitations.

## 4. Strong older formulations deliberately NOT restored

These were rejected because the stronger wording outran the current evidence or collapsed distinctions.

### Rejected as overstrong

- "Truth-discriminating mesorah was lost centuries ago."
  Reason: current evidence shows possible loss models and current non-completion, not that historical truth-discriminating tradition globally ceased.

- "Only circular reasoning avoids the discriminator conclusion."
  Reason: a non-circular internal traditional discriminator can succeed; circularity itself can also be benign when it contains independently warranted asymmetry.

- "Mesorah contains falsehood" without qualification.
  Reason: safe only when the operative broad mesorah category is explicitly stipulated to include both incompatible claims under classical non-contradiction.

- "Canonical textual tradition does not reliably preserve a stable text" as a global claim.
  Reason: Re'em/variant-recension evidence does not support that universal generalization.

- "No admissible package can complete TRACE" over every possible package/bridge.
  Reason: the current result is restricted to R_current, the routes actually recovered and independently licensed.

- "All disagreement mechanisms fail."
  Reason: some candidate traditional discriminators succeed in principle and positive controls are essential to the paper.
