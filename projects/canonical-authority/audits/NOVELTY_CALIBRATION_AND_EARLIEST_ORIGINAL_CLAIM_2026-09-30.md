# 1. Section 2.1 — fixed target comparison

## Prior manuscript framing

> The paper's first methodological claim...

## Literature calibration

The core move is established comparative methodology.

Oliver Freiberger explicitly describes comparative studies as involving comparands and a `tertium comparationis`, the point or question with regard to which the items are compared. His methodology also stresses historical-empirical description of comparands in their own contexts before juxtaposition.

Therefore:

[
	ext{heterogeneous comparands} + 	ext{fixed comparison point}
]

is not an independently novel claim of this paper.

## Current disposition

**ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND / ADOPTED METHOD**

Paper-specific use:
the tertium is a fixed truth-apt Rashi-Ramban proposition pair and the comparands are heterogeneous Jewish authority/knowledge mechanisms.

That use may contribute to the final novelty intersection, but fixed-target comparison itself is not claimed as new.

## Prior manuscript framing

## Literature calibration

The general idea is also established.

Contemporary epistemic-authority theory treats authority as relative to a specific domain of propositions. Arguments from expert opinion likewise ask whether the authority's expertise is relevant to the field or proposition at issue.

Therefore the bare claim

[
	ext{authority in domain }D_1 
otRightarrow 	ext{authority on proposition in }D_2
]

is not independently novel.

## Current disposition

**ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND / PAPER-SPECIFIC APPLICATION**

The paper uses this established domain sensitivity to classify Jewish mechanisms by native output and ask whether any licensed relation transfers their force to (C_S).

# 3. Section 3 — source reconstructions

The individual source reconstructions are substantive scholarship, but the current evidence does not justify historical-priority claims for the following observations taken separately:

- pluralism/legitimacy is not automatically descriptive truth;
- provenance classification is not automatically proposition adjudication;
- institutional authority can coexist with represented error;
- hierarchy needs a reliability bridge;
- authenticated prophecy requires target coverage;
- received creation knowledge does not automatically include every exegetical proposition in the same comment.

These may be important applications and combinations, but each has substantial adjacent literature.

**SOURCE-GROUNDED APPLICATION / SYNTHESIS**

No broad novelty claim belongs here before final source and comparator closure.

# 4. Section 4 — route architecture

This is the first location at which the paper can responsibly begin a bounded originality claim.

## Component precedents

The individual jobs have precedents:

- source relevance / field relevance: arguments from expert opinion;
- proposition-specific support: ordinary evidence and argumentation theory;
- evaluator recognition/warrant: epistemic authority and expert-identification literature;
- multiple premises jointly supporting one conclusion: linked-premise argumentation;
- independent reasons, aggregation, and defeat: convergent argumentation and formal/informal aggregation literature.

Therefore TRACE, DISCRIMINATE, RECOGNIZE, package support, and COMPOSE should not be presented as though each were invented from nothing.

## Paper-specific synthesis

The proposed contribution is their integration for this problem:

[
	ext{native source reconstruction}
	o
	ext{source-attributable TRACE}
	o
	ext{rival-specific DISCRIMINATE}
	o
	ext{evaluator-relative RECOGNIZE}
]

with:

[
	ext{package TRACE} 
eq 	ext{COMPOSE}
]

and explicit attribution of outcome-material bridge/composition information.

The reviewed comparator corpus has not yielded a direct predecessor for this exact integrated architecture applied to heterogeneous religious-authority mechanisms at one fixed truth target.

# 9. Answer to “Am I the first?”

Current evidence supports:

> No direct predecessor has been located in the reviewed comparator corpus for the integrated architecture and worked result.

Current evidence does not support:

> I am definitely the first person in the history of scholarship to make this claim.

The second formulation requires much stronger historical exhaustiveness than the project currently has.

This does not weaken the paper. Peer-reviewed novelty is normally established by a precise delta against the closest literature, not by proving that no human being has ever formulated any overlapping idea.
