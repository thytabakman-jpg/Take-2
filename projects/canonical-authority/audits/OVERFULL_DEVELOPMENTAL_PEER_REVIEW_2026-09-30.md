## Developmental additions made in this pass

### 1. Why this case is worth studying

Added an explicit defense of the Rashi-Ramban case as an analytic microscope / stress case rather than a statistically representative sample.

The case is diagnostically useful because:
- the truth target can be frozen;
- both rivals possess canonical standing;
- Ramban directly attacks a supporting premise;
- several traditional epistemic categories sit close to the case;
- proposed authority routes can be tested proposition by proposition.

### 2. PD demonstrated rather than merely named

Added a concrete account of what PD actually changed:
- legitimacy vs truth;
- provenance vs proposition coverage;
- explanation vs adjudication;
- source-package combination vs case-level composition;
- source content vs selector information;
- canonical survival vs evidence of truth.

This makes PD a visible research heuristic rather than unexplained branding.

### 3. RECOGNIZE defended as a distinct stage

Added a direct argument that:
- a genuine evidential relation can exist without a later evaluator having warranted access to it;
- strong social recognition does not substitute for target linkage;
- evaluator-specific warrant does not make truth evaluator-relative.

### 4. Architecture ablation

Added a stage-by-stage ablation table showing the specific mistake made possible by removing:
- PROFILE;
- TRACE;
- DISCRIMINATE;
- RECOGNIZE;
- the separate COMPOSE boundary.

This turns the architecture from a stipulated taxonomy into a functionally testable decomposition.

### 5. Bridge-regress closure

Added an explicit answer to:
"What licenses the rule that licenses the bridge?"

The answer uses provenance and non-forcing:
- source-attributable bridge;
- independently defended external bridge;
- commitment-relative bridge;
- unresolved further dependency -> conditional/indexed/unresolved output.

No infinite selector hierarchy is required.

### 6. Human-readable microcases

Added minimal examples distinguishing:
- authentic but target-irrelevant;
- target-relevant but symmetric;
- discriminatory but evaluator-inaccessible.

This gives the route architecture intuitive counterexamples rather than relying on formal notation alone.

### 7. Key terms defined

Added explicit working definitions of:
- canonical;
- truth-apt;
- evaluator.

This protects against scriptural-canonicity confusion, naive "fact" language, and evaluator-relative truth misunderstandings.

### 8. Major non-claims made explicit

- tradition is generally unreliable;

- every machlokes is factual;
- modern scholarship automatically outranks traditional sources;

### 9. Concrete bridge-provenance types
