## Method

Each paragraph GOAL consumes a paragraph-local MT packet first.

For each paragraph:

[
MT(P_i)
	o
GOAL(P_i).
]

The MT packet identifies the paragraph object, its reader relation, and the highest-value local question independently of the current wording.

The GOAL packet then records:

- **Goal** — what the paragraph is actually trying to make true for the reader;
- **Success** — observable completion condition;
- **Protect** — meaning that cannot be lost;
- **Current** — current draft-state relative to that goal;
- **Reopen** — evidence or movement change that reopens the paragraph goal.

This is a repository-grounded semantic run. No claim of promoted native tool runtime is made.
